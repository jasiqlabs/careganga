## 2. Concrete ownership, scopes, lifecycle and source decisions

**Provider** is a registered legal provider organization (hospital group, clinic practice, lab business, etc.). **Center location** is one actual provider operating site. Neither equals CG-02 Foundation branch. One provider can have many locations; one center account sees only explicit verified assigned locations. CG-01 manages password sessions, capability grants, sensitive-operation password confirmation and role assignment; **CG-14 adds the verified account↔center affiliation fact/actual own-center resource checker** as US-001 registration proceeds, then CG-01 authenticates that scope for each permitted operation. A Foundation-affiliated representative with an existing CG-01 password account may edit a PENDING application for own location only; **pending representative never inherits Center Admin or Foundation role**, and no center context appears automatically because account email matches a provider contact.

**Professional doctor** is one CG-14 identity with license/registration jurisdiction+number, specialties/qualifications and protected evidence. Doctor is NOT automatically a CG-01 staff account or a CG-15 patient. One doctor may be approved at multiple center locations, and fees/service modes are particular to doctor-at-center. The doctor identity verification is independent of association approval: change at Center A cannot rewrite Center B fees or revoke a doctor globally unless an authorized global professional suspension is real. A global suspension does suppress all public doctor affiliations. CG-01 grants/CG-14 center assignment must both permit center actions. Identity/qualification registration numbers are private or minimally validated badge-only; never publish raw registration proofs, personal mobile unless explicitly designated public professional contact, bank details or reviewer identity.

**Independent status axes:**
- Provider: DRAFT/SUBMITTED/RETURNED/REJECTED/VERIFIED/SUSPENDED/DEACTIVATED; location: PENDING/VERIFIED/INACTIVE/SUSPENDED; *account* active/suspended through CG-01 and real center affiliation; *public* NOT_PUBLISHED/PUBLISHED/WITHDRAWN separately.
- Doctor (implemented US-003 source): PENDING/SUBMITTED/VERIFIED/SUSPENDED; original CG-03 case separately carries RETURNED/REJECTED/IN_REVIEW. Doctor-at-center affiliation: PENDING/APPROVED/SUSPENDED; public eligibility requires current **both** verified doctor and center + approved affiliation and valid current consent/fee. A verified provider is not automatically publicly published; a published center never bypasses current provider suspension.
- A CG-03 FINAL_APPROVED decision alone is not a CG-14 VERIFIED or PUBLISHED record: CG-14 verifies source revision/digest/decision, scope and current restrictions and commits the guarded transition. Returned evidence edits create **new frozen submissions**. Replayed/old decision cannot apply to changed profile/fee or rights. On suspend, revoke public status **transactionally in CG-14** and deny new CG-15 booking eligibility via a current typed source check; actual CG-15 appointment changes occur only after CG-15 exists. A suspended center must not be publicly discoverable.

**Approval source adapters introduced in the *later owner story***: Initial CG-03 has a real CG-01 privileged-role-change process, **not** a magical provider case. CG-14 US-002 introduces provider/location process-source registration and frozen revision+digest case inside CG-03; US-003 introduces DOCTOR_VERIFICATION, US-005 own-center CONTENT_CHANGE. CG-03 owns case records/reviewer assignment/process versions/decisions. CG-14 owns provider/doctor/content records and guarded application of final decision. Prior CG-03 original processes remain functional after changes. CG-19 original file owner has initial CG-01/03 files, not necessarily provider draft refs: **CG-14 US-001 introduces a limited pre-review provider/location draft source adapter inside CG-19**, then US-003 adds doctor registration evidence, US-005 center public photo/FAQ source. CG-19 controls upload intent, actual file bytes, MIME/size/scan, private signed access, checksum, content rights; no copy of originals or bank scans inside CG-14 database.

**Financial boundary:** CG-14 stores sensitive provider bank/settlement destination reference, masked view and authorized version history only for future CG-17 settlement, with encryption/access restriction. It never executes collection, bank transfers, donor settlement or patient invoice, which belong to CG-17/09. Doctor fees quoted here are informational current center-specific consultation/follow-up prices, not patient paid invoices. Future CG-15 holds a verified immutable fee revision for accepted appointments without altering CG-14 historical fees.
