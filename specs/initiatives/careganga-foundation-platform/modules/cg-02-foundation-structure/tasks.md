Outcome: Maintain one genuine Foundation legal/contact profile, real geography and Foundation- or active-branch-scoped departments/committees with authorized current CG-01 account heads and committee participants, including audit/history; future employee/member/project/budget links remain assigned to later owners, not invented now.

Legal profile, actual geography, Foundation-wide department/committee and active-branch-scoped department are real CG-02 records; future CG-04/05/09/10 linked masters remain later-owner integrations.

Invalid level, duplicate root area/name, inactive/closed branch, cross-scope parent/cycle, unauthorized participant and stale edits are rejected without mutation. The original U21–U22 theme, safe permission-based selector, clear user messages and redacted audit are mandatory.
