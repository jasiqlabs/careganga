## 2. Cross-screen shell and interaction patterns

### 2.1 Global navigation observed

- **Left sidebar:** CareGanga Foundation logo/brand, searchable navigation field, categorized hierarchical menus, nested/expandable groups, selected-menu styling, a bottom-left **Super Admin** identity/status area. The active item is blue in the captured interface. Sidebar can be considerably longer than the browser height and scrolls.
- **Top shell:** current module title and control-panel subtitle, global search for *name / ID / mobile / email* with a Ctrl+K hint, notification bell, inbox/message badge, **View Site** link, help icon, user avatar, **Super Admin** name/online indicator and account dropdown.
- **Main workspace:** KPI cards across the top; filter/search panels; tabbed lists; action toolbars; data tables; detail/right-hand information cards; quick actions; charts and recent activity panels.
- **Form patterns:** right-side drawers (staff, role, reward, CMS record, payment and allowance), centered modal dialogs (volunteers, notification, media/gallery, payroll components), larger wizard pages (branch, employee, campaign, voucher, budget, fund, invoice), and inline preview panels (letters, IDs, CMS banners).
- **Actions repeatedly visible:** Add/Create, Edit, View/Eye, Copy/Duplicate, Download/Export, Preview, Save Draft, Save, Next/Previous, Submit for Verification/Approval, Approve, Reject, Send Back/Forward, Refresh, Clear/Reset, Search/Apply Filters, Print, Share, Publish/Hide, Delete, Bulk Actions and pagination where relevant. **VERIFY:** any protected action must have actual permission and state validation; screenshot appearance alone does not establish implementation.
- **Statuses repeatedly visible:** draft, pending review/verification, approved, rejected, active/inactive, completed, returned, paid/pending/released, published/hidden, employee probation/on-hold, and scheduled/sent notice states. Final PRDs must use state names consistently per module, rather than copying conflicting labels between screens.

### 2.2 Admin surface versus public content

**Admin controls business records**, while CMS controls which appropriate records/content are published. Screens of branches, roles, applications, vouchers, salary and personal HR information are internal. Project/campaign/NGO information and explicitly authorized team profiles can be published. The screenshots' **View Site**, **Preview Website**, publish toggles and CMS previews are evidence of the intended Admin-to-public-site relationship.

**Do not treat the screenshots as a ready-made UI specification to clone.** Reuse their actual business fields and affordances while grouping and simplifying repetitive filters, overloaded sidebars and fragmented financial/HR navigation. Operational source ownership follows the master BR, not the old menu categories.

## 8. Explicit UI/UX specification extracted from the screens

### 8.1 Elements to preserve as user capabilities

1. **Context-rich but unified dashboards:** KPI cards, summary charts, status distribution and quick actions. Each metric links to the correctly filtered source list where permitted.
2. **Search and filtering:** Common keyword search, several dependent filters, Clear/Reset, Apply/Search, saved filters where needed, status tabs, date ranges, exports and pagination. Avoid duplicating the search form on every tab.
3. **Persistent context:** Selected record detail beside list (field visits, people, donations, HR, payroll, etc.) and linked right-side approval/summary cards where data and screen size allow.
4. **Multi-step wizards:** Branch, campaign, employee, budget, fund, voucher, payroll and invoice show a stage/stepper, required/optional fields, Draft/Previous/Next/Submit, side summary and responsible workflow.
5. **Role-appropriate actions:** View/Edit/Approve/Reject/Return/Export/Publish are shown only where grant and business status permit, with backend verification and meaningful reason/status.
6. **Preview-before-commit:** The strongest recurring pattern is live preview for website hero/public project, official letter, ID card, public NGO page and invoice/receipt. Preserve preview integrity, not pixel-copy.
7. **Controlled publication:** Distinct **Save**, **Submit for Review**, **Publish**, **Hide/Unpublish**, and public appearance; changes to a master record do not automatically publish confidential information.
8. **File handling:** Photo, identity document, supporting evidence, receipt, letter attachments, PDFs, media resource and signed/received copies. Upload buttons and previews should reuse a secure shared file component.
9. **Status transparency:** Draft, pending, verified/approved, rejected, active, paid, issued, sent and due are visibly distinct. Show progression and reviewer history rather than only generic toast messages.
10. **Scalable lists:** Large admin lists use bounded pagination, filters, sort and export; avoid unbounded DOM/table data on small devices or slow connections.

### 8.2 UI/UX issues and recommended improvements (analysis, NOT user-provided contractual requirements)

- **Navigation overload:** The old sidebar has dozens of nested items including overlapping payroll, letters, allowances, finance and content; regroup by user job/role and show only relevant items. Preserve features, **not the old clutter**.
- **Densely packed full-screen dashboards:** Too many KPI/cards/filters/side widgets make important pending actions hard to find. Use one focused primary workflow, collapsible advanced filters and a contextual details drawer.
- **Misleading visual state:** Several screenshots show loading placeholders or demo-like empty cards. An empty record set, a loading state, a permission denial, unavailable backend and failed save must be visually distinguishable.
- **Inconsistent entity duplication risk:** Roles/employee registration/payroll setup, project/program/CMS teaser, donation/payment receipt, fund/budget/ledger are displayed in different sections; unify source entities and link rather than copy.
- **Unclear "approval" vocabulary:** Approving an application, verifying a document, approving salary, issuing a letter and publishing a page are different business transitions. Use exact action verbs and status badges.
- **Screenshot sample content is not product data:** Dates, numeric KPI counts, names, sample providers, generated transaction strings, legal/certificate IDs, and publication copy visible in the screenshots are not design defaults.
- **Color and visual polish:** Blue sidebar, white cards, colorful status chips, purple/blue actions, colored graphs, compact type are reference aesthetics only. Modernize color contrast, spacing, keyboard navigation and focus, preserving clarity and CareGanga branding.
- **Responsive design:** Drawer/wizard summaries must become an accessible collapsible summary on mobile; high-density tables need alternative responsive row/detail layouts, not tiny horizontal shrink-to-fit spreadsheets.
- **Financial and patient privacy:** Personal contact, Aadhaar/PAN, bank, salary, clinical and support records must not be exposed in screenshots, public previews, external shares or unrestricted exports.
- **Stateful drafts and confirmation:** Submission of review, deletion, rejected record, role revocation, payroll release, refund and publication changes must have appropriate confirmation/error/undo or safe corrective operation; screenshot button does not prove it.
