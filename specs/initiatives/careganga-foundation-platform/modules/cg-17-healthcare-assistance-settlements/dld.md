## Concrete healthcare finance and merchant contracts

**Single canonical schema:** The exact names and invariants are specified under **§4A Implementation-ready ownership, schema and payment proof**, below. Do not create the older planning aliases `assistance_policies`, `patient_charges`, `center_collections` or `foundation_liabilities` as additional tables: use only `healthcare_policy_revision`, `patient_charge`, `center_collection`, `assistance_award`, `foundation_liability`, `center_merchant_binding`, `provider_settlement`/`provider_settlement_line`, and `cg17_financial_effect`. Where §4A uses singular names, they are the sole authoritative real schema objects; other narrative/plural business terms are just concepts.

**Versioned policy:** One reviewed currently effective consultation policy applies only to an actually eligible verified CG-14 provider/doctor/center and real CG-15 patient/service. Standard eligible consultation assistance is 50% Foundation / 50% patient subject to amount cap; a protected exception for 100% remains pending until genuine authorized approval. The current accepted booking retains the historical policy revision.

**Immutable accepted charge:** Validate the CG-14/15 price/source, currency and current patient eligibility; persist `gross_minor = support_minor + due_minor` and the true accepted fee/policy revision in `patient_charge`. The same service booking and unknown future optional ERP stock/source cannot generate a duplicate charge.

**Separate verified collections and Foundation payable:** CG-17 merchant binding is real center-owned payment gateway verification, not CG-14's bank settlement destination. `center_collection` verifies real patient money through the applicable original center; cash attestation is restricted and audited. `foundation_liability` requires actually completed eligible service plus approved `assistance_award`, not booking/paid status. CG-09 posts Foundation-only effects exactly once using the original `cg17_financial_effect`, while CG-19 issues original receipt/document after genuine evidence.

**Monthly provider settlement:** `provider_settlement` and its immutable source `provider_settlement_line` enforce paid ≤ approved outstanding, source reversal/adjustment and partial-period reconciliation with real protected CG-14 bank destination. Distinguish gross center service value, center patient collections, Foundation approved payable and Foundation actually settled. No assumed partner commission, automatic center transfer, hidden duplicate finance ledger or direct Foundation receipt from patient money.

**QA:** Simultaneous duplicate callbacks, wrong merchant/center or currency, unverified on-site proof, full coverage exception and cap rounding, pay-at-center due, canceled/refunded or never completed case, concurrent partial settlement, older-client retry, repeated month close, cross-center negative scope, missing CG-09 acknowledgment and restored outbox agreement. Current source QA definitions in `tasks.md` cover these gates; actual DB/payment-device exercises remain NOT_RUN.

## 4A. Implementation-ready ownership, schema and payment proof

**Amount precision:** Store accepted INR money as PostgreSQL BIGINT integer paise with CHECK(amount >= 0) and explicit ISO currency, never floating-point JS amounts. Verify original CG-14/15 quoted fee and legally approved tax breakdown; CG-17 must not invent GST. After deterministic minor-unit rounding and support cap, require `gross_minor = support_minor + patient_payable_minor`, `support_minor <= gross_minor`. Store accepted fee revision and policy revision immutably; an edited fee/policy never silently reprices past bookings. Reject mismatched original center, doctor, patient and currency.

**Owned relational schema / uniqueness:**
- `healthcare_policy_revision(root, center, optional_doctor, service_kind, revision, valid_from, valid_to, approval_state, percent_or_fixed, cap_minor, approved_case_ref, actor, audit_ref)`: one unambiguous effective precedence (specific doctor over center) and no overlapping **approved effective** rules at the same scope/priority; protected 100% exception needs a real CG-03 case and original CG-17 finalizer, not a client toggle.
- `patient_charge(root, center, actual_patient, source_kind, source_ref, optional_line_ref, accepted_fee_revision, policy_revision, gross_minor, support_minor, due_minor, currency, accepted_at, state)`: UNIQUE(root,source_kind,source_ref,line_ref), with genuine delivered CG-14/15 owner verification. Protect the accepted source snapshot rather than copying optional future service masters.
- `center_merchant_binding(root, center, provider, provider_merchant_ref, protected_credential_ref, verification_state, revoked_at)`: **CG-17-owned US-003 merchant enrollment and verification** for one real CG-14 center; unlike CG-14's protected bank settlement destination, this is a center-owned gateway merchant, not a Foundation collection account. Token/secret stays in approved server vault or encrypted reference, never Web/native/logs; disabled or unverified merchant means online checkout unavailable.
- `center_collection(root,center,charge_ref,attempt_ref,method,external_payment_ref,evidence_ref,amount_minor,currency,status,verified_by,verified_at,revision)`: UNIQUE real provider+merchant+callback/payment identity and UNIQUE bounded manual idempotency key; append-only proof/adjustment for center-owned patient collection. Authorized cash attestation with durable receipt may verify cash; an unconfirmed UPI/card screenshot alone cannot verify electronic payment.
- `assistance_award(root,charge_ref,approved_support_minor,approved_case_ref,policy_revision,state,revision)` and `foundation_liability(root,center,source_kind,source_ref,award_ref,service_completed_at,amount_minor,currency,state)`: each eligible **completed** original source creates at most one approved contribution liability, regardless of patient collection status; a merely booked/no-show/unapproved source creates none. A later cancellation uses a compensating reversal, never deleting history.
- `provider_settlement(root,center,period,currency,revision,approved_total_minor,paid_minor,adjusted_minor,state)` and `provider_settlement_line(settlement_ref,liability_ref,applied_minor,reversal_ref)`: unique original effect assignment, bounded remaining balance, approved partial payment, one genuine CG-09 disbursement source. Provider bank destination still comes from restricted CG-14.
- `cg17_financial_effect(root,effect_kind,source_kind,source_ref,source_revision,idempotency_key,amount_minor,currency,original_cg09_ref,posting_state)`: UNIQUE original source/effect/idempotency; durable bounded outbox/claim for independent original CG-09 post and reconciliation. No duplicate CG-09 general ledger or universal payments framework.

**Isolation, transitions, and concurrency:** Every record stores current Foundation root and relevant provider center; use actual prior-owner FKs only when those source tables exist plus typed public facade authorization at all read/options/export/write paths. Defend with unique/check/FK and optimistic revision or scoped row lock, not frontend validation alone. Implement explicit DUE→PARTIALLY_CENTER_COLLECTED→CENTER_COLLECTED, PENDING_PROVIDER→VERIFIED/FAILED/REFUNDED, APPROVED_LIABILITY→PARTIALLY_SETTLED→SETTLED/REVERSED transitions as separate axes. For source completion-vs-cancellation, close/settlement and double refund races, lock only the affected source/settlement and commit audit+effect atomically. No external provider call inside a Postgres lock.

**Authenticated online payment:** US-003 first validates a verified current center-owned merchant binding. A Razorpay callback must verify signature, merchant/provider, charge owner root+center, original attempt, exact source amount/currency and valid transition. Duplicate, late, out-of-order or wrong-center events are idempotent and cannot mark paid or change Foundation books; a client redirect or screenshot is never proof. Ambiguous provider outcome stays PENDING_RECONCILIATION, with capped targeted retry/requery and no always-on center polling. Authorized refund verifies actual payment owner and writes a linked compensating effect.

**Foundation posting separate from patient money:** Only a completed/eligible original appointment (or a genuinely enabled approved optional service) plus approved CG-17 support creates a liability. The CG-17 transaction writes that liability and durable source event once; the CG-09 facade posts Foundation liability/payment with same immutable source event key, returning its true journal ref or PENDING until verified. Missing/ambiguous CG-09 callback must be reconciled against the same key before another attempt. A center patient receipt never creates CG-09 Foundation income. Actual settlement requires protected destination, authorized approval, partial balance and bank payment evidence; no invented partner-commission formula.

**Typed source and UI routes:** Implement `quote`, `acceptCharge`, `verifyOwnCenterMerchant`, `startCenterPayment`, `recordManualCenterCollection`, `verifyProviderEvent`, `approveAssistance`, `accrueCompletedService`, `settle`, `reverse`, `readSafeBenefit` with versioned DTO/outcome codes and current CG-01 actor+root+center+patient purpose. Foundation Admin Web, own-center Portal Web, Public Web/native Public Android and permitted Internal Android each use the same owner API and CareGanga tokens; **no separate Center Android app**. The CG-18 editorial publisher may consume a public-safe approved policy projection but owns its page/revision/cache, not CG-17 money rules.

**Independent CRITICAL QA gate (NOT_RUN):** Disposable PostgreSQL migrations/rollback/restore, partial unique/idempotency/currency/index proofs, concurrent source booking-completion-cancel/refund and monthly partial settlement, wrong-center webhooks, data-scope-negative list/options/export, stale policy, lost CG-09 response, zero-work worker/egress and original H16/H17 Web/native responsive visual source trace. Every case must be traced to its owning task; no CI/GH Actions or premature runtime/clinical/financial certification.

## Monetary balance and settlement invariants

**Every eligible calculation** snapshots policy version, accepted fee, patient obligation, Foundation share, center merchant, and evidence. Currency decimal rounding policy is deterministic; reject negative/capped/invalid benefit and gross mismatches. Persist patient collection and Foundation payable as separate source ledgers with one unique immutable financial-event id per payment/adjustment. Verified patient receipt is a center receipt, not automatically Foundation cash. Unverified provider callbacks yield PENDING/RECONCILIATION, not PAID.

**QA:** Given a versioned eligible consultation price, compute full/percentage/capped Foundation share with rounding and enforce patient_due + foundation_share = accepted_total. Center merchant collection must never silently create Foundation cash; verified external payment, refund and center invoice/reconciliation are distinct. Two settlement claims or webhook retries cannot settle one event twice. A later policy change does not rewrite an earlier booked snapshot.

**Owner integration:** Requires actual CG-14 fees, CG-15 booking/service source, CG-09 Foundation ledger and CG-19 document proof. Integrate payment adapter only for verified owner merchant; unavailable provider leaves payment pending, not artificially settled.
