## Original business ownership and non-negotiable split

**CG-22 owns one genuine medicine catalogue and each verified partner's approved price revisions,** independently of inventory ERP. With operational ERP enabled only, CG-22 also owns its partner's medication batches, opening receipt, stock/reservation/issue/adjustment movements, suppliers, purchase/GRN, same-partner-location transfers, expiry/quarantine/recall and bounded owner reports. **No duplicate business masters:** CG-14 owns provider organization, approved center/location, service approval, licensing evidence, staff-center affiliation and PHARMACY_ERP operational grant; CG-01 roles/access; CG-03 original independent review cases when required; CG-19 document bytes and communications; CG-12 Foundation stock/suppliers; CG-09 Foundation journals; CG-15 consented patients; CG-17 actual eligible patient charge, benefit and Foundation settlement. Partner inventory and GRN are **not** CG-12 warehouse data or CG-09 Foundation purchases, patient cash, or CG-17 reimbursable completed service.

**Default OFF is a backend safety invariant:** Deployment capability `CAREGANGA_ERP_PHARMACY_ENABLED` is `false` by default; missing, misspelled or malformed values are OFF. Operational permission requires ALL of: module+schema actually present and independently approved/verified for enablement; exact backend global ON; current CG-14 ACTIVE VERIFIED license-ready pharmacy center/location and independently APPROVED effective `PHARMACY_ERP` center grant; current CG-01 effective action + authenticated permitted center; real stock/service prerequisites; required professional supervision/evidence for protected actions. Recheck at **every** private list, options, detail, create/modify, download, export, job and retry. A global ON never activates another center or bypasses a suspended grant. Frontend merely reflects actual backend effective capability, never enables operations by optimistic local flag. OFF removes all CG-22 ERP operational menus/APIs/exports/jobs (including stock-history routes) but preserves immutable source data. Only original CG-21 may provide separately authorized minimal audit-event metadata, never a hidden CG-22 OFF bypass; US-001 price catalogue/enquiry and all core healthcare consultation services continue unchanged.

**No expanded pharmacy sales service:** Core published medicinal pricing is informational, not proof of availability, patient eligibility, licensed dispensing, prescription, medication delivery, cart, sale/checkout, tax result, wholesale transaction, clinical advice or retail invoice. Under ERP ON, a stock `ISSUE` is an auditable internal stock-out reason under licensed responsible supervision where required, **not** a patient prescription fulfillment or cash sale. No medicine stock movement alone creates a CG-17 patient benefit/center invoice/CG-09 Foundation cash or FR-087 settlement. **Expired, recalled or quarantined physical stock remains on-hand for audit but has zero issuable available quantity. Each verified partial transfer reception is separately immutable and replay-safe.** Such financial link would need a separately authorized **real completed payable service** and CG-17 existing policy with original source adapter in the actual initiating owner; it is NOT invented by this module.
