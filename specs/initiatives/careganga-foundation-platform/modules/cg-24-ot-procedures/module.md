## Original business boundary and default-OFF policy

**CG-14 is the sole source for partner center, published service/approved effective price, verified professional affiliation, license/center identity, public service directory/contact and `center_operational_capability(OT_ERP)`.** CG-24 US-001 adds procedure-specific, approved information attached to an **existing real CG-14 procedure service** (typical duration/required qualifications/suitability disclaimer, not a second fee table), with genuinely published safe procedure details available while ERP OFF; CG-18 retains editorial website publication/layout rather than procedure source. CG-24 US-002–005 own **optional private OT operations only**: OT rooms, actual physical assets/readiness, verified qualified-team requirements, patient-consented procedure reservations, authentic pre-/in-/post-operative source events, legal medical notes, report aggregates and narrowly conditional CG-17 completed-service financial signal. CG-15 alone owns patient identity and consent; CG-19 alone owns private file bytes/notifications; CG-03 original protected approval case if real policy needs it; CG-01 role/auth; CG-09 actual Foundation journal; CG-17 original patient charge/eligible benefit/center collection/assistance/settlement; CG-20 private cross-module MIS; CG-12 Foundation inventory. No duplicate CG-14 doctor/price/center master, CG-15 patient, CG-19 file store, CG-09/17 finance ledger, staff payroll, hospital-wide EMR, e-prescription, insurance claims, patient procedure self-checkout or anaesthesia/clinical guidance algorithm.

**Default OFF is strict:** `CAREGANGA_ERP_OT_ENABLED=false`; missing/malformed/unrecognized flag resolves false. All **CG-24 US-002–005 private operational** list/options/detail/create/edit/export/download/job/worker/actions require effective backend global ON **AND** actually present approved code/schema and clinical enablement **AND** real ACTIVE VERIFIED CG-14 center's approved current `OT_ERP` grant **AND** current CG-01 actor+action+exact root/center/appropriate clinical purpose **AND** lawful professional/facility/readiness/CG-15 consent when relevant. A center's approved service and global ON alone never authorize the first OT booking. OFF hides operational UI and refuses all operational endpoints/jobs (including CG-24 record history/export); does not delete prior records. Independently authorized original CG-15/19 patient clinical release and CG-21 minimal audit metadata are separate owners, not a CG-24 OFF bypass. CG-14 public service enquiry and genuine other core features run with all four ERP flags OFF.

**Illustrative commercial/clinical content not executable facts:** Example H14 metrics, an advertised 30% nursing/OT benefit, room status, patient-case completion and example partner payout are not proof of real readiness, clinical work, patient payment or Foundation liability. No direct e-commerce checkout or unconditional payment. Verified completed applicable service may be signaled by a **US-005 adapter into existing CG-17 only** when CG-17 genuinely approves this center/service/patient policy, accepts original fee and verifies source completion; CG-09 posts only Foundation payable. Center patient collections remain separate.
