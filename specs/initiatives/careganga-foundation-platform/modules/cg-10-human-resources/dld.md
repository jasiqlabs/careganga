## 2. Source ownership, employee and account lifecycle

**Applicant versus employee versus login:** CG-10 US-001 may enroll an HR-created application with no CG-01 login; or safely link an **existing actual** CG-01 account with verified identity and current permission. **Never create a duplicate employee login automatically from form text**. A PENDING application is not an ACTIVE employee and cannot have an official activated employee ID, internal staff role or payroll entitlement. Freeze an application snapshot and CG-19 validated evidence, introduce genuine CG-03 **EMPLOYEE_ENROLLMENT** source case/process *in CG-03* within US-001, pin process version, source revision and digest. Source steps follow configured approvers; original B27/B29 shows Registration → State Verification → Zonal Head → HR Verification → Super Admin Approval → Employee Activated, but **the actual policy is versioned and may have valid stages/roles different from screenshot labels**. Missing required reviewer, process or validated evidence yields PENDING/UNAVAILABLE, never a fabricated final approval.

**Final approval/application:** CG-10 observes genuine CG-03 final approved decision for exact current immutable submitted source/digest; short guarded PostgreSQL transaction creates **one** approved `hr_employee`, unique scoped employee number, and authorized assignment; duplicate decision key returns original accepted employee. If account grant is necessary, perform a typed CG-01 effect/fence in existing owner; CG-10 may propose an allowed role but only CG-01 can actually grant one, respecting current built-in security/MFA/root separation. **Do not show staff ACTIVE or a usable privileged login until both employment and any required CG-01 account/grant are confirmed**; if CG-01 effect cannot atomically finalize, durable PENDING_ACCESS + on-demand/bounded reconciliation and fail-closed scoped reads. Approved employee can exist with no staff login when the job legitimately does not require login; no phantom account/auto ROOT.

**Actual employment identity and real branch:** An employee has one CG-10 relationship and a history of effective-dated CG-02 branch/department+line-manager assignments. Current branch must be **real ACTIVE**, department valid in that branch or genuine Foundation scope, manager must be actual approved eligible employee with lawful scope (no self, cycles or resigned supervisor), and normalized employee code is unique in root. A CG-02 Foundation branch is never a healthcare center. An employee can have current CG-01 staff login but access to a branch is gated by **current** employment assignment and CG-01 explicit scoped role; neither alone authorizes a write. Transfer revokes old branch access at its actual effective time, with true CG-01 permission transitions and source audit; historical HR reads remain scoped to current delegated HR grants, not an old login.

**Privacy:** HR legal name, parent names, DOB, gender, blood group, Aadhaar/PAN, private mobile/contact, address, bank/IFSC/account/UPI, resume, identity, photos, qualification and compensation proposal are **restricted**. Separate safe employee directory/selector (name, public work designation, actual branch/department/status, employment code only when current grant permits) from protected private HR profile read, HR-document reviewer view and finance-authorized bank/compensation proposal. Do not store raw Aadhaar in logs/public projection or cache; tokenize/encrypt sensitive bank and identity fields and use CG-19 protected file references, not raw uploaded files/PDFs in CG-10 DB. PII retention and employment/legal policies are client/independent compliance review items; do not assume automatic biometric or Aadhaar verification.
