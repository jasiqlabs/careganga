## Specific confirmed shared-source limitation (not merely a UI issue)

1. In merged CG-02 `BranchSourceFacade.projectBranchScope`, `projectBranch`, `financeReportScope`, `requireFinanceActorBranch`, current rights generally reduce to protected `foundation.branches.admin` OR `branch.manager_account_ref`. The **master BR expressly lists delegated state/district/block coordinators and branch/department/project/finance/HR staff**; a manager-only per-branch projection does not demonstrate that source contract. CG-01 can assign capability-bearing roles but that is not a verified current record/branch grant. **CG-MB-001 P0** remains open. Do not invent a broad `allowAll` for accountants/stockkeepers to hide it. Resolve design/source owner CG-01/02, then verify CG-09, CG-12, CG-04/05/10 and further consumers. General geographic scope above branches (state/district/block) must be expressed by approved owner roles/area membership, not a fabricated client filter.
2. CG-12 `InventoryCatalogService.requireScope` uses the same manager/root source and CG-12 operations check both locations; this is a **confirmed consumer dependency** (CG-MB-003), **not proof of cross-location stock corruption**. CG-09 `FinanceReportsService.verifyFilterScope` and CG-02 `financeReportScope` deliberately fail closed for a non-root branch-only actor, and CG-20 `MisDashboardService` also avoids silent multi-branch aggregate. These are good guard patterns; unverified jobs and non-manager delegation still need owner QA.
3. CG-14 `HealthCenterSupportScopeFacade` checks actual affiliations, and CG-15 center patient intake / CG-17 monthly settlement use this separate center source. Do **not** add CG-02 branch FKs to a center-only clinical table or automatically let an internal branch manager read all patients of a nearby clinic. The *same human* may be a staff account + center professional with separate explicit assignments and narrow patient-consent purpose.
4. In the active CG-07 branch alone, `DonationAdminService.list/exportCsv` now allow ROOT `ALL` / `FOUNDATION` / approved ACTIVE branch with authorized source filtering; new `3319_cg07_donation_branch_provenance.sql` prevents donor-money branch retarget. This is source-authored, not independent SQL verified, not current main. Historical CG-09/CG-19 origin must stay attached after manager/project/campaign change.
5. CG-19 file owner source, CG-20 scheduled report/outbox and CG-18 public publication have potential stale-recipient/source and cross-scope consequences; **CG-MB-008/009/011** are **VERIFICATION_GAP**, not proven data leaks. The correct failure action is to trace real source and fix only confirmed bypass, with a test.
6. For future CG-06/08/11/13/22/23/24 the receiving owner must decide per row/command `FOUNDATION_ONLY`, `FOUNDATION_OR_CG02_BRANCH`, `CG14_CENTER`, `PATIENT_OWN`, or mixed; add same-root, current-grant and immutable source guards. Root/branch and center scopes are separate, and global ERP disabled status is not a permission. Do not activate a future dependency just because this review document exists.
