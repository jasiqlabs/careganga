- **Non-negotiable restriction:** An approved alias by itself gives neither original account access to the other account's appointments/files nor staff permissions. Any future clinical aggregation would require the separately authorized patient/consent/file owner, not reusing this review. This is the safest explicit non-destructive review under the original story; it is not a destructive physical merge.
