## 2. Concrete ownership, scopes, lifecycle and source decisions

**Provider** is a registered legal provider organization (hospital group, clinic practice, lab business, etc.). **Center location** is one actual provider operating site. Neither equals CG-02 Foundation branch. One provider can have many locations; one center account sees only explicit verified assigned locations. CG-01 manages session, capability grants, MFA and role assignment; **CG-14 adds the verified account↔center affiliation fact/actual own-center resource checker** as US-001 registration proceeds, then CG-01 authenticates that scope for each permitted operation. An invited provider representative with actual account may edit a PENDING application for own location only; **pending representative never inherits Center Admin or Foundation role**, and no center context appears automatically because account email matches a provider contact.

**Professional doctor** is one CG-14 identity with license/registration jurisdiction+number, specialties/qualifications and protected evidence. Doctor is NOT automatically a CG-01 staff account or a CG-15 patient. One doctor may be approved at multiple center locations, and fees/service modes are particular to doctor-at-center. The doctor identity verification is independent of association approval: change at Center A cannot rewrite Center B fees or revoke a doctor globally unless an authorized global professional suspension is real. A global suspension does suppress all public doctor affiliations. CG-01 grants/CG-14 center assignment must both permit center actions. Identity/qualification registration numbers are private or minimally validated badge-only; never publish raw registration proofs, personal mobile unless explicitly designated public professional contact, bank details or reviewer identity.

**Independent status axes:**
- Provider: DRAFT/SUBMITTED/RETURNED/REJECTED/VERIFIED/SUSPENDED/DEACTIVATED; location: PENDING/VERIFIED/INACTIVE/SUSPENDED; *account* active/suspended through CG-01 and real center affiliation; *public* NOT_PUBLISHED/PUBLISHED/WITHDRAWN separately.
- Doctor: DRAFT/UNDER_REVIEW/RETURNED/VERIFIED/REJECTED/SUSPENDED/INACTIVE; affiliation: PENDING/APPROVED/PAUSED/ENDED; public eligibility requires current **both** verified doctor and center + approved affiliation and valid current consent/fee. A verified provider is not automatically publicly published; a published center never bypasses current provider suspension.
- A CG-03 FINAL_APPROVED decision alone is not a CG-14 VERIFIED or PUBLISHED record: CG-14 verifies source revision/digest/decision, scope and current restrictions and commits the guarded transition. Returned evidence edits create **new frozen submissions**. Replayed/old decision cannot apply to changed profile/fee or rights. On suspend, revoke public status **transactionally in CG-14** and deny new CG-15 booking eligibility via a current typed source check; actual CG-15 appointment changes occur only after CG-15 exists. A suspended center must not be publicly discoverable.

**Approval source adapters introduced in the *later owner story***: Initial CG-03 has a real CG-01 privileged-role-change process, **not** a magical provider case. CG-14 US-002 introduces provider/location process-source registration and frozen revision+digest case inside CG-03; US-003 introduces DOCTOR_VERIFICATION, US-005 own-center CONTENT_CHANGE. CG-03 owns case records/reviewer assignment/process versions/decisions. CG-14 owns provider/doctor/content records and guarded application of final decision. Prior CG-03 original processes remain functional after changes. CG-19 original file owner has initial CG-01/03 files, not necessarily provider draft refs: **CG-14 US-001 introduces a limited pre-review provider/location draft source adapter inside CG-19**, then US-003 adds doctor registration evidence, US-005 center public photo/FAQ source. CG-19 controls upload intent, actual file bytes, MIME/size/scan, private signed access, checksum, content rights; no copy of originals or bank scans inside CG-14 database.

**Financial boundary:** CG-14 stores sensitive provider bank/settlement destination reference, masked view and authorized version history only for future CG-17 settlement, with encryption/access restriction. It never executes collection, bank transfers, donor settlement or patient invoice, which belong to CG-17/09. Doctor fees quoted here are informational current center-specific consultation/follow-up prices, not patient paid invoices. Future CG-15 holds a verified immutable fee revision for accepted appointments without altering CG-14 historical fees.

UUID internal keys remain hidden from UI; timestamps are UTC instants; local business hours store IANA zone at each center, day+local clock time. **Every table and all cross-table joins constrain current root and the verified entity relationship**, not merely the API controller.

**Single publication source:** `health_center.operational_state` is live operational validity, `health_center_publication` is the ONLY effective center-public listing/visibility authority, `health_doctor_center_publication` the ONLY doctor-at-center public authority, and `health_center_content_publication` the ONLY current published presentation revision. Do not also save a mutable `health_center.publication_state` or a polymorphic public visibility shadow table: every published status is derived from one FK-backed current pointer plus currently eligible source. The `declared_capability_status` is a verified descriptive service statement, NOT CG-15 appointment slot availability, CG-16 real lab-test sale, or CG-17 financial benefit. 

**Composite-FK and fee DB proof:** Every referenced CG-14 parent table/revision/affiliation must expose UNIQUE(root,id), and doctor_center and doctor_revision also expose UNIQUE(root,doctor_id,id) for the doctor-specific published-pointer join; a revision additionally exposes UNIQUE(root,source_id,id) and the published pointer uses **both** root+source+revision reference, preventing cross-center or cross-root revision attachment even when a UUID is guessed. No single polymorphic visibility row without FK is permitted. For fees prefer a PostgreSQL `btree_gist`-backed EXCLUDE constraint on root+doctor_center+fee_kind+approved tstzrange overlaps, with actual parent row lock and matching transaction as a second defense. If extension is unavailable, independent database reviewer must approve and demonstrate an equally strong serializable/locked mutation plus concurrency SQL test before any production-ready claim; runtime only-optimistic precheck is insufficient.

**Additive migration plan:** (1) inspect actual delivered CG-01 realm/account and CG-02 geography, CG-03 case and CG-19 file interfaces/ORM before deciding exact relational FK targets; (2) use one versioned CG-14 migration sequence, CREATE TABLE + UNIQUE(root,id) on **all** referenced parents and real composite same-root FKs (provider→center, center→affiliation/hours/flags/content, doctor→revision/affiliation, association→fee, submitted revisions→source, center/doctor-center/content actual published pointer→immutable source revision). (3) create only indexes for approved root+status/current/public and bounded doctor/center search/geography; (4) install CG-14 source adapters **inside existing owner packages** in US-001/002/003/005 respectively and review their migrations independently; (5) no default doctors/locations/provider aliases or future CG-15/16/17/18 FK; (6) restore on disposable PostgreSQL database from backup, test up/down/forward repair, FK/unique/partial/exclusion constraints, current index scans and compatibility with actual CG-01/02/03/19 flows. Existing real data upgrades require expand→safe backfill→verify→contract with backup before any destructive operation. Actual SQL, backup, restore, EXPLAIN and independent specialist signoff **NOT_RUN**.

**Indexes/throughput:** root+provider+status, root+center+state/district/PIN, root+doctor normalized name/specialty, root+doctor_center+current validity, root+fee association/fee_kind/effective date, root+public visibility/generation for only eligible data. Cursor+limit (server-capped) and trusted sortable fields; avoid full-table scans per keystroke/pin, source-select N+1, broad geometry index before volume needs it, per-provider/center idle worker. The one-root application is not permission to leak cross-center private records.

**Financial / source consistency:** No FK to not-yet-implemented CG-17 settlement or CG-15 appointment; no CG-16 clinical test price row. Fee changes/version validity protect later CG-15 accepted quote: a future booking references immutable accepted fee revision; if no CG-15 booking exists it must not block fee edits. Bank destination is not proof that funds moved. CG-19 has original evidence ownership; CG-03 owns original case decisions; neither is copied into health revision as alternate approval record.

## 5. Approval, transaction, privacy and cross-center race fences

**US-001 first real draft:** Foundation CG-01 authorized admin creates provider+location in a short same-root PostgreSQL transaction after verifying genuine CG-02 area; idempotency unique(root,requestKey,payloadDigest) returns original accepted result on retry. An invited center representative can submit own pre-review attachments through CG-19 adapter before CG-03 provider process exists. Proof invalid/scanning stays NOT_READY/PENDING, not automatically verified.

**US-002 verification:** Freeze provider/location structured profile and CG-19 validated proof digest. CG-03 process adapter is added **inside CG-03** for real provider/location, gets a fixed source revision/digest and actual configured authorized reviewer; one authenticated final CG-03 source decision can be consumed by CG-14 via owner facade. Under short DB root+record lock verify decision/current restriction and commit CG-14 review state, unique final decision application key and audit; if CG-03 proof freshness can't be checked transactionally, use a reviewed revocation/version fence and fail closed, not a stale approval. Activation of center account requires real CG-01 affiliation/grant transition; publication is a **separate authorized CG-14 action**, not a side effect of approval. Return/new evidence invalidates old submission. Suspension clears CG-14 current public eligibility and notifies CG-01 effective affiliation guard; public read checks current source state to avoid stale cards. No network provider calls while holding DB lock.

**US-003 professional/fee guard:** Doctor license normalizes jurisdiction+registration and checks partial unique index and genuine evidence. Concurrent attempts create at most one original doctor identity per verified registration. Multiple center affiliations have independently granted owner access and a real current status. Fee edits acquire short lock scoped (doctor_center,fee_kind), validate nonnegative exact minor units, ordered effective timestamps and no overlapping APPROVED effective ranges; immutable original accepted price remains referenceable for future CG-15 booking. A doctor suspended at one center disappears only there; globally suspended doctor disappears from all public affiliations. A future CG-15 schedule/slot API remains outside CG-14 and is **never called in first US-003**.

**US-004 publication and revocation:** Public query computes `provider VERIFIED & active & center VERIFIED & active & approved affiliation & doctor VERIFIED & publication PUBLISHED & valid source rights` at read time using bounded authorized projection. Public route is a **CG-14-owned Next.js + native Public Android directory**, runnable with no CG-18 CMS page. Only if CG-18 later exists does the already approved CG-14 public DTO appear inside the CG-18 editorial page slot; CG-18 cannot edit CG-14 registration, fees or verification. Unpublish/suspension revokes pointer/status under one DB transaction and invalidates public generation/SEO/caches.
