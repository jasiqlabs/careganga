## Operational prerequisites, not unanswered product questions

1. **Real money:** Foundation/center bank ownership and provider payment credentials must be verified before enabling online patient collection or center settlement. If not configured, show only an approved pay-at-center option (where the care provider actually supports it); do not claim a Razorpay payment succeeded.
2. **Real clinical access:** Foundation must collect provider/doctor verification material; consent and secure report access must be operational before viewing or sharing medical documents. No self-issued accreditation or prescription signature is assumed.
3. **Statutory/financial documents:** Only legitimate client/accountant-supplied GST, 80G, bank and legal details may be published or calculated. Any legal retention, tax or healthcare regulatory point must be checked by the responsible business/legal authority before launch.
4. **Third-party providers:** SMS/email/WhatsApp, payment gateway, map and cloud storage keys and provider agreements are implementation/onboarding inputs, not reasons to create duplicate product modules.
5. **Capacity and infrastructure:** The 100-user/performance/availability/RTO/RPO values in CG-BR-600 are proposed targets until realistic sizing and hosting are verified; security, correctness, reconciliation, isolation and truthful failure states are unconditional P0 requirements.
6. **UI scope:** English is default. No additional languages, location-screen questionnaires or full offline sync are introduced merely for completeness. Recorded geography fields remain source-backed business records.
