## Business owner and isolation

**Owns:** One Foundation profile, root-scoped real geography hierarchy, branch registrations/activated branches **and versioned current branch location/contact/manager/media maintenance preserving immutable approval history**, **US-001 Foundation-only departments/committees and US-002 post-activation branch-scoped departments/committees**, existing CG-01 account participants, plus submitted branch-to-Foundation-department links finalized at activation. **Implementation prerequisites (scheduled before CG-02; not yet actually delivered):** CG-01 identity + CG-03 genuine approval decisions + CG-19 protected evidence for seven-step branch submission. **Later owned integrations, not prerequisites for this module's core path:** CG-10 employee heads, CG-05 projects, CG-04 members, CG-09 budgets and CG-18 public listing link only from their delivered source owners. Only real validated records may populate selectors; later owner supplies cross-module implementation and its own QA. No shadow master or fake operational status.

**State and invariant:** Foundation identity/area/department/committee and real CG-01 account participation are usable before any branch; a branch-scoped department requires an actually active branch. Registration drafts have revisioned submitted snapshots and append-only CG-03 case history: DRAFT → SUBMITTED → RETURNED/REJECTED or FINAL_APPROVED for the **matching** submitted revision; only a Super Admin final CG-03 decision authorizes new-branch activation. CG-19 owns genuine document evidence. Only one idempotent owner finalization can activate/code the approved branch, and approval alone is not active status. Active/inactive/closed branches preserve history and do not silently delete linked departments. Geography never invented. Every list/search/option/detail/export/write must validate actor action plus the **actually existing Foundation/geography/branch scope**; CG-02 does not assume or confer future center/patient/finance visibility. Privacy and truthful end-user states are mandatory.
