Absent CG-17, CG-15 records CG-14 accepted gross consultation amount **as immutable snapshot display**, payment collection is `DUE_AT_CENTER/NOT_VERIFIED`; no Foundation contribution assumption, cash journal, gateway, invoice or paid receipt. - **CG-16/22/23/24 (future optional ERP):** consume `getConsentedPatient(root,patientRef,purpose,actor)` only when actually implemented and respective deployment+center flags active; they create **their own** internal orders/visits/procedures/stock, never CG-15 consultation reservations. **Pattern choice:** versioned simple **schedule+exception policy**, **PostgreSQL doctor-global exclusion/short doctor lock + center capacity check** and small **booking/queue state guards**, source-adapter/CAS and short transaction Unit of Work. Reject two independent path schedules, in-memory slot locks, rechecking only on frontend, duplicate identities, a universal booking engine for Lab/OT/nursing, event-sourced EMR, separate booking microservice or speculative distributed saga. Worker exists **only** for real due holds/notifications with bounded index/leases.

## 3. End-to-end state and API design by story

### US-001 — H07 doctor-center schedule, leave/block exceptions

`GET /api/v1/appointments/admin/schedule?doctorRef&centerRef`, `POST/PUT /schedules`, `POST /schedules/:ref/weekly-windows`, `POST /schedules/:ref/exceptions|leave`, `POST /exceptions/:ref/submit-review`, `POST /exceptions/:ref/apply-approved-decision`, `GET /availability/doctor/:ref`. Current CG-14 doctor list maps real center associations and specialty; actual CG-01 current Foundation role / CG-14 own-center operator + proper CG-03 protected reviewer for leave. H07 selected doctor, weekday toggle, 09–13 + 16–20 windows are **demo** until configured, duration and max slots policy-validated (no invented default). Time windows store **local weekday + timezone**, convert candidate dates to UTC at query; invalid time zone/ambiguous DST or duplicate windows must fail or explicit selection, not silently shift. Review approved blocked range against current booked patients; do not cancel confirmed reservation. Copy-to-other-center previews each real CG-14 association and explicitly explains that any same-time in-person schedules are mutually exclusive location choices. The global doctor booking reservation still permits only one active hold/confirmed consultation at that time; conflicting VIDEO, time zone and unauthorized targets fail closed. Leave not approved => not a booked-slot cancellation. Include no synthetic provider record or H07 example physician.

### US-002 — two public journeys, one occupancy and genuine CTA

`GET /api/v1/appointments/public/doctor/:ref/center-options`, `GET /availability/center-first?doctorRef&centerRef&date`, `GET /availability/slot-first?doctorRef&date` and `GET /booking/eligibility?doctorRef&centerRef&slot`; both resolve **the same** approved CG-14 doctor-center fee+schedule exception+global doctor occupancy. Center-first chooses doctor→center→date/slot; doctor/date-first asks date/time then returns **all** valid center choices for that doctor-time and makes patient choose one; **never auto-choose** from overlapping centers. H07 current availability calendars not a reservation; cached availability is advisory, final booking rechecks. CG-15 US-002 mounts current `Book Appointment` into real CG-14 provider detail and existing CG-18 approved placement only if genuine verified current doctor/schedule/route exists. A suspended doctor/center immediately loses CTA and any deep link is unavailable. No CG-17 finance or patient registration required to browse.

### US-003 — one patient, verified contact/consent and secure files

`POST /api/v1/patients/registration`, `POST /patients/existing/claim` with a valid current CG-01 password-authenticated account and independently established patient consent/purpose, `GET /patients/me`, `PATCH /patients/me` (CAS), `POST /patients/:ref/consent`, `POST /patients/provisional` own-center limited reception, `POST /patients/duplicate-review` protected admin, `GET /patients/authorized-options` returns minimal labels only. Actual sensitive profile fields from BR10, optional CG-19 evidence (photo, patient-provided report, prescription **upload** not doctor e-prescribing), real CG-02 geo values if relevant. Generate one nonguessable **internal id** and separate unique **display Virtual Patient ID** on verified/accepted lifecycle; do not copy H15 mock numbering. A phone may be shared among family and may change: matching verified phone alone does NOT allow patient merge or reading another patient's private record. Staff assisted provisional patient only with recorded intake/authorization; self-account linking requires verified proof. Approved merge is a separate reviewed old→new alias/provenance with immutable appointment/document FK reconciliation and fraud checks, never deletion/re-key of protected history by UI edit. Patient consent is versioned purpose; consent withdrawal blocks new disallowed disclosure without erasing legally retained audit.

### US-004 — atomic doctor-wide hold → confirmed consultation and fee DUE

`POST /api/v1/appointments/holds`, `GET /holds/:ref/status`, `POST /api/v1/appointments/confirm`, `GET /appointments/:ref`; accept exact current **patient, doctor, CG-14 association and location, real available time, mode, reason and accepted fee/consent** with expected source version, idempotency key and immutable payload digest. On hold under root+doctor lock: expire elapsed HELD rows by verified state transition, reject unapproved leave/block and provider status, assert no active `doctor_time_reservation` overlapping candidate in-person interval using DB exclusion; check configured center mode capacity under same lock; insert one protected hold/global reservation. On confirm, lock same root+doctor+hold+patient, reverify real CG-14 effective fee, source digest, current patient consent/eligibility, unexpired hold and absence of conflicting independent center booking; atomically `CONFIRMED`, booking ID and immutable fee. If fee changed since review, return explicit **PRICE_CHANGED_NEEDS_REVIEW**, do not silently accept; a new hold may be required, no ghost reservation. Replayed same request returns original after source query; uncertain write returns **UNKNOWN_WRITE_STATUS** and re-read original source key before recharging/reserving. If CG-17 absent, `collection_display_state=DUE_AT_CENTER` and `payment_mode=PAY_AT_CENTER`, no automatic paid/subsidy/online option. Genuine CG-17 later owns payment/benefit and confirmed financial callback; late positive online capture after expired hold **must not resurrect an overlapping slot**, route to actual CG-17 verified refund/manual resolution. Both public Web/native and staff Center Portal call one guard, not two schedulers.

### US-005 — original CG-19 booking slip, ID/QR and private retrieve

`GET /api/v1/appointments/me/:ref`, `POST /appointments/:ref/issue-slip`, `GET /appointments/:ref/calendar.ics`, `POST /appointment-verification/check` with rate-limited opaque status-only token, `POST /appointments/recover` verified identity/secondary CG-01 proof. CG-15 owns readable booking display ref and source version; **CG-19 owns immutable template, PDF/print/QR token file/issuance/reissue/revocation and actual send**. D-07 print: two copies within one A4 **only if actually PAID_VERIFIED online**, one patient copy for PAY_AT_CENTER, center staff permitted to reprint own copy; no fake online receipt. QR public reveals only minimal active validity/time window and no symptoms, patient name/phone/Virtual ID/file/account; center scanner rechecks actual own-center relationship and role before additional minimal booking context. Invalid/revoked/expired/cancelled tokens not accepted as check-in. Calendar .ics includes real doctor/center/date/time and no symptom/diagnosis/location tracking/private signed URL. No policy to expose Booking ID as public bearer retrieval.

### US-006 — center-scoped reception, queue and genuine visit history

`POST /api/v1/visits/check-in`, `POST /visits/:ref/advance`, `POST /visits/:ref/no-show`, `GET /visits/center`, `GET /visits/patient-own`. Valid current booked appointment may be looked up through a correct logged-in center/qualified receptionist and verified booking details **without a CG-19 slip requirement**. Each check-in acquires root+center+local-date/token issuance lock, validates current CG-14 association and patient, inserts one queue source or reuses replay, transitions `CONFIRMED→ARRIVED→WAITING→IN_CONSULTATION→COMPLETED` with verified actor and revision. No-show only from allowed not-arrived slot under appropriate configured time policy; not arbitrary early no-show. Canceled booking never enters new queue, late arrivals show explicit review, two clerks same visit yield one token. Own center staff see only necessary own-center current attendance/status; Center A cannot see Center B clinical/medical reports, Foundation Admin restricted aggregate, patient sees own progress where approved. Without actual provider notes owner, no EMR, prescription authoring or diagnosis log.

### US-007 — safe reschedule, cancel and financial owner effects

`POST /api/v1/appointments/:ref/reschedule`, `POST /appointments/:ref/cancel`, `GET /appointments/:ref/change-history`. Validate current patient ownership OR own-center/approved CG-01 permission, allowed business windows and active CG-14 doctor. Reschedule **locks old appointment and doctor old/new in deterministic sorted order**, then atomically holds/reserves new doctor/date/location and releases old active doctor reservation upon successful new confirmation; if new missing/blocked, old confirmed booking and accepted fee unchanged. If doctor changes, lock both identities sorted; no double-booking across centers. A reschedule **may require new approved CG-14 fee** and explicit patient acceptance before old reservation release; history preserves old source snapshots and reason. Cancellation transitions booking once, releases actual occupied slot/queue eligibility and writes one audit. Original CG-17, if previously paid, alone creates and verifies applicable refunded amount; CG-15 shows `REFUND_PENDING` only if real source exists, never refunds by enum or creates bank journal. CG-19 notification queued after source commit with genuine per-channel delivery state; provider outage doesn't roll back valid cancellation and duplicate notification events deduped.

### US-008 — own patient ID/history, protected reports and scoped source reports

`GET /api/v1/patients/me/overview`, `GET /patients/me/appointments`, `GET /patients/me/documents`, `GET /appointments/admin/summary`, `GET /appointments/center/report` with source-authorized cursor/date/status/doctor/location filters and safe export. Works after real US-003 patient even with zero booking and no CG-16/17/22/23/24; provides own virtual patient ID/consent/profile, and only actual recorded CG-15 appointments/CG-19 original slips. Real CG-17 verified receipt/payment state and real CG-16 professionally released report show **only** after owner exists and current patient/clinician-purpose policy permits; generic patient report tab cannot query unreleased results. Doctor patient clinical access requires actual current professional care relationship/current scope/permission, not just doctor credential. No clickable “Prescriptions”, “Family Members”, lab/nursing/OT booking/medicine order without separately real approved source (D-08/D-26). Admin overview may show H15 referral count only where actual records exist, never screenshot example totals.

## 4. Channel, security and NFR evidence gates

**Foundation Admin Web:** H07 full authoring/oversight and H15 patient/bookings filtered list, selected private patient detail and source status, authorized staff actions, role/grant navigation. **Center Portal responsive Web:** only verified own CG-14 center doctor schedules, patient assisted registration/consent, appointment/queue/status and permissioned center report/print. **Public Website/native Public Android:** same center-first and slot-first current eligibility, verified mobile patient create/existing claim, confirmation/pay-at-center, slip/QR/calendar, cancel/reschedule and own ID/history with equivalent business options/status, not WebView. **Native Internal Android:** only actually granted CG-03 doctor-leave approval/assigned center intake or check-in/visit actions if authorized; full H07/H15 Admin settings/patient directory **NOT_APPLICABLE** under D-17. No automatic Center Portal Android duplicate. All share the original CareGanga design token and task-navigation semantics, with native-specific accessibility.

**Permissions:** Default-deny on current CG-01 **actor + action + root + CG-14 center scope + patient consent and purpose**, on EVERY list/option/detail/QR/print/file/approve/write/export/status report. Patient login is not implicit CG-01 staff role, center affiliation not Foundation branch, prior consultation at a center not lifelong unrestricted clinical history. Strict PII/security/rate-limit/size/clinical audit, never raw phone/diagnosis/OTP in logs/caches, use purpose-scoped patient report access/trace/retention. Public booking id/reason cannot be guessed. Revoked doctor/center active status invalidates NEW bookings immediately but retains old legal history/cancellation rules. Fail states `VALIDATION/NO_ELIGIBLE_CENTER/NO_AVAILABLE_SLOT/PRICE_CHANGED_NEEDS_REVIEW/CONFLICT/UNAUTHORIZED/NOT_FOUND_OR_HIDDEN/UNAVAILABLE/UNKNOWN_WRITE_STATUS` with real UI guidance; NEVER fake “booked”, “paid”, “report ready” or “notification sent”.

**Concurrency/worker:** In-person doctor-wide PostgreSQL GiST exclusion plus verified doctor lock and bounded active holds (expiry state transitions before new overlapping insert); center capacity under same transaction; idempotent source-key and actor scoped unique constraints. Only indexed real due HELD rows/CG-19 delivery attempts may be worked by short CAS claimed leases; when no pending events, **zero tenant scan, no continuous per-center provider traffic**. Explicit finite retry/reconciliation after crash/provider timeout; unknown booking/paid response always requery original source before new write. Record committed audit, report current slot availability after write, avoid full-range time slot pre-generation at scale.
