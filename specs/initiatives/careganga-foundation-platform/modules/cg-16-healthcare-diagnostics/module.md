## 1. Business decision: partnership catalogue core, clinical Lab ERP optional

**Core default OFF:** `CAREGANGA_ERP_LAB_ENABLED=false` (missing/malformed also FALSE). **US-001** owns genuine test master, sample type, category, approved test/package composition and effective published per-center test price/discount; **US-003** projects real source-selected public lab menus and pricing; **US-004** introduces lab-specific enquiry/contact/available-test CTA into the **existing actual CG-14 verified provider page** and optional CG-18 editorial placement. These three must start and work **when Lab ERP is OFF**, with no CG-15 patients, orders, blood/specimen storage, CG-17 cash/discount sponsorship, CG-19 report or CG-20 MIS. A verified CG-14 center can be discoverable under CG-14 alone, even when CG-16 master does not yet exist. **CG-14 owns provider legal entity, location, verification, license, lab center activation/public directory and general service category/capability**; CG-16 does NOT duplicate CG-14 center/service master or provider record. The public lab enquiry can be sourced from real current CG-14 approved public contact info, never directly enroll a patient or create an order.

**Optional build but GLOBAL OFF:** **US-002** is the actual Lab ERP internal order, sample custody, analysis processing, professional result review and **licensed release** at verified CG-14 current `LAB_ERP` center, for genuine CG-15 verified patient/care purpose + original CG-19 private file/notifications. **US-005** independently owns bounded lab equipment/maintenance/quality/compliance evidence at verified CG-14 lab, **not** generic CG-12 inventory or analyzer/LIS. Both require: real deployed code and migrations, global flag ON, current CG-14 license/readiness+explicit center capability `LAB_ERP`, CG-01 exact authorized actor/center, and actual patient consent where the operation involves an identifiable patient. They must deny NEW operational orders/edits/workers/API/menus when OFF. Historical **already actually released** report remains accessible to its verified owning patient/legitimate clinician via separate purpose-specific CG-19+CG-15 authorization even when ERP now OFF; legal correction/retention is a tightly reviewed exceptional source process, not automatically re-enabled Lab ERP.

**Finance boundary:** US-001 per-center price/discount is the **real partner's published commercial catalogue price**, not CG-17-approved Foundation benefit, paid receipt, commission or CG-09 journal. CG-17 later owns true verified patient collection/foundation contribution/partner payable if needed; until then US-002 has truthful `PAYMENT_NOT_VERIFIED/AMOUNT_DUE`, never `PAID` from saving a lab order or "report delivered". No Foundation gateway collects a center patient's amount automatically. Public service cards show approved price only and no speculative discount claim.

**Privacy:** CG-15 owns only consented patient identity/account link and patient history; CG-16 owns **real lab order and specimen/result state**, not a second patient or generic appointment. CG-19 owns private original PDF/files/document ACL/issuance; CG-03 owns configured approval cases (not a substitute for licensed lab professional review inside CG-16). CG-18 CMS only owns editorial content placement; CG-20 MIS only reports actual owner views; CG-22/23/24 have their own ERP, no need to install. No patient diagnosis prediction, analyzer integration, lab inventory/reagent procurement system, self-service lab checkout, e-pharmacy, broad EMR, insurance or fabricated medical reports.
