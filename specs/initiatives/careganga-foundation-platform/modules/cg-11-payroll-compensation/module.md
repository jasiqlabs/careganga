## 1. Canonical owner boundary — one payroll, one ledger

**CG-11 owns:** configurable, approved versioned payroll policy and allowance/deduction/statutory rule versions; approved `employee_compensation` effective history and restricted employee payout destination revisions; Foundation-root/eligible-branch period payroll source; frozen CG-10 attendance+leave source snapshots with version digest; each deterministic payroll run/line/arrears/correction; linked real CG-03 payroll review source; payment batch/instruction source/status *without new bank/accounting master*; exact original CG-09 finance effect reference; source-provided payslip content/private employee read and CG-11 scoped payroll summaries/history.

**CG-10 owns:** actual employee registration/active status, legal/branch/department employment assignments, approved attendance/leave, HR promotion/transfer/exit proposals and source revision; no CG-10 payroll salary editor/payable or payroll paid flag. First eligible CG-11 salary setup works with a genuine CG-10 active employee **without a promotion request**. CG-11 applies future approved CG-10 HR salary proposal only after its own current payroll authority/effective-date approval. Payroll does not create a second employee.

**CG-03 owns:** final approval case/steps/decision. CG-11 **introduces** PAYROLL_STRUCTURE_CHANGE and PAYROLL_RUN_RELEASE original source+process adapters into the delivered CG-03 typed engine when its real payroll proposal exists; protected final batch release follows D-05 Super Admin authority, separate proposer/reviewer, current MFA and pinned source revision. No new CG-03 table/approval service.

**CG-09 owns:** original Foundation/branch cash/bank account master, balanced journals, expense/payable/payment evidence, verified bank/cash transactions and finance reconciliation. CG-11 introduces typed payroll source into existing CG-09 financial owner and never directly writes journals, creates a bank master, or claims PAID from bank-file ACK. **Payroll's restricted employee payout destination is not the Foundation cash_bank_account master:** keep versioned, masked/encrypted, controlled per-employee authorization inside the CG-11 compensation instruction source and use original CG-09 Foundation settlement bank/account and verified result. Holds/reserves are payroll statuses or **CG-09** recognized earmarks only, never another CG-11 fund balance.

**CG-19 owns:** original protected salary-slip PDF/template/content hash/file bytes, private signed download, send-notification status. No CG-11 PDFs/upload store or email sender; a document can show approved salary while payment remains pending, but must never say PAID before original CG-09 verification. Regenerate/print/share doesn't create another payroll liability or payment. **CG-21** renders current permissioned read-only CG-11 source audit (not new CG-21 payroll log table). **CG-20** later consumes limited approved safe aggregate, not a prerequisite or a second payroll report engine.

## 2. Three independently testable vertical stories

### US-001 — Approved effective compensation, bank instructions and payroll settings

With current CG-01 root/branch/payroll role and real active CG-10 employee, create first compensation from original B43 + C02/C03/C11–C14: salary-component/allowance/deduction/tax *configuration where independently authorized*, monthly/period/frequency, employee eligibility, effective dates, overtime/leave/hold/arrears rules, masked controlled payout destination and expiry/revision. Block invented Indian statutory percentages/automatic tax filing: calculation rule/salary policy and applicable statutory provider data must be supplied/approved by authorized Foundation/accountant, with explicit unsupported/unconfigured state. CG-03 case only for policy-required sensitive approval; first setup works without CG-09 posting, CG-19 payslip, or future CG-20. Source audit and role-safe Web plus scoped own account/approved review routes on native. **20 manual QA cases**.

### US-002 — Deterministic payroll run, review and immutable approval

With approved current CG-11 compensation and actual eligible CG-10 employee+attendance/leave/policy version, follow original C01/C04/C05/C06 wizard: choose CG-11-policy-derived Foundation fiscal label/branch/period/cycle **without requiring a CG-09 financial-year FK until US-003 posting**, select eligible employees, validate absence/holds/arrears, calculate exact gross/net/withheld and source snapshot, allow restricted adjustments, review+CG-03 approval and lock one run revision. Use original financial-year semantics/closed period and one current `run` per root/branch/period/approved cycle; never duplicate pay obligation, mix unlike employee periods or silently override approved run after late CG-10 attendance correction. A late approved correction enters a **new separately approved arrears/adjustment** source linked to original run, not a second same-period salary. True reporting for draft/approved without CG-09 payment; CG-19 payslip and CG-20 MIS not required. Private authorized Web plus **native reviewer approval** where D-17 role grants, and own-pay no premature final amount. **20 QA**.

### US-003 — Controlled reserve, payment batch, payslip, final dues and history

With actual CG-11 approved run/revision and CG-03 approved release, collect only current eligible per-employee payout destination (no public bank data), create idempotent batch and original CG-09 payable/payment request; verify each bank/cash outcome independently (PAID_VERIFIED / FAILED / UNKNOWN / PARTIALLY_PAID / REVERSED), record one original CG-09 balanced posting/payment per real payment source. A provider ACK, bank file creation or single success among many is not the entire batch paid. Partial/unknown re-queries the original transfer attempt key; only verified failure/partial permits a separately authorized, uniquely keyed outstanding-balance attempt under one due amount lock. Don't replay confirmed salary. Approved original line may generate private CG-19 payslip, with payment status independent and regeneration revision-safe. Include C07/C08 pay history/export, source-only C09/C10 audit exposure through CG-21, payroll-specific C01 dashboard without CG-20, and **verified final dues** read-only status to original CG-10 B48 exit clearance. Foundation Web Admin and current permitted native reviewer **approval+own-pay/payslip** screens use common source/authorization; public and healthcare-center systems have **no salary features**. **20 QA**.
