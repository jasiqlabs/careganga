## 3. Screen detail: profiles, assistance and reporting

**Intake/verification:** One readable name and private contact/location, lawful optional gender/age/beneficiary type (choose 'Not provided' rather than infer sex/child age), actual CG-02 area hierarchy, usable no-project state, optional genuine permitted CG-05 projects with validity and no dummy entries. Preserve clear **Draft / Pending Verification / Under Review / Verified / Returned / Rejected** and separately **Active / Inactive** lifecycle; a rejected *application review* is not the same as person deletion. A case needing CG-03 review shows genuine approval history/reviewer remarks, with scoped CG-19 proof and no fake Verified. Duplicate candidate shown as masked prompt to permitted staff; do not auto-merge shared phone family records. Search by ID/name/contact may only return within current scope, private data masked on list. All genuine edit/save/conflict/cancel and accessibility states must exist.

**Assistance:** A compact original-style form/drawer/sheet, not a new app aesthetic. Choose a current approved beneficiary; valid aid category/material/cash, qty/unit/value/actual source, approval, responsible staff, optional linked project/activity/field visit. If material, show **real** CG-12 item/warehouse available/held quantities only to role entitled to that store; if cash, show real CG-09 voucher/disbursement status, never a fabricated financial balance. Confirm dialog states amount/recipient and business consequence. Show Requested, Under Review, Approved, Pending Stock/Finance, Partially Delivered, Delivered, Returned/Corrected and Unavailable with separate action availability. A request not yet fulfilled has no green Delivered. Provide original signed receipt/evidence only when CG-19 and real authorized source exist. Return/correction references original delivery; historical rows immutable and readable. Safe lost-response action: *Checking previous delivery—do not submit again* until original source reference resolves, with refresh.

**Reports:** A07's six KPI cards reflect genuine source and meaningful privacy conditions. One beneficiary linked to two projects contributes one to Total Beneficiaries and two to Project Associations if separately labeled. Pending/denied aid cannot inflate assistance distributed, payment spent or inventory issued. Include 'Data unavailable' rather than presenting zero after CG-09/12 failure; accurate as-of date/source freshness and status legend. A07 campaign filter is shown in original but must not return imaginary campaigns; show only legitimately linked CG-07 source or hide/deactivate with human explanation until usable. Category, gender and child counts may be aggregated only when lawful data and privacy policy allow, with unknown/not-provided values not silently excluded. Only approved public aggregate goes through CG-18; de-identify and suppress tiny cohorts to avoid re-identification.
