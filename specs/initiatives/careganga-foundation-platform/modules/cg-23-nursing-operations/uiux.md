## 1. Original CareGanga brand, H13 interaction structure and current state

Preserve the **original CareGanga logo**, dark navy main sidebar, bright-blue selected entry, clear grouped healthcare navigation, compact light-background cards and white header, dense but readable data tables, controlled filter bars and meaningful status chips and responsive right-side detail. In the indexed H13 original, nurse/booking/ongoing/completed/pending KPI cards, State/District/Center/Service/Date filters, booking list with contextual right-side patient+nurse details, and **Service Details / Nurse & Assignment / Care Plan / Visit & Progress / Documents** tabs are explicitly evidenced. Do not copy mock booking totals, 'Completed' badges or patient names from screenshot as actual data. Use actual source-owned labels/empty/failure/permission statuses. No fabricated nurse map/location heatmap, internal patient plan summary on public site or auto-assign animation.

**Global feature split:** With all four ERP flags OFF, only CG-14 genuinely published nursing-center/service/price/enquiry appears via existing Foundation Admin, Center Portal and Public Next.js / native Public Android. The Nursing ERP sidebar, private Nurse Master, Schedule, Assign Service, Care Plan, Visit, Reports, exports and jobs are **absent** under backend `CAREGANGA_ERP_NURSING_ENABLED=false`; a protected Admin deployment status may display 'Nursing operations are not enabled' to specifically entitled configuration staff, not a nonfunctional menu for ordinary users. Foundation Admin/Center Portal and native Internal Android never infer activation from a frontend switch. One global ON is not permission: current CG-14 NURSING_ERP grant, qualified professional, patient consent and CG-01 action/purpose remain mandatory.

## 2. H13 source-to-story screen and state contracts

### US-001 — Verified nurse professional and per-center affiliation

**Center Portal:** Nurse Master search/filters by readable nurse name, qualification, verified center, valid credential/approval status. **Add nurse** captures only actual required qualification, licensing issuer/jurisdiction/registration number, bounded competence category and original CG-19 evidence reference through protected upload. Do not ask for technical root, generated ID, redundant center input when current center context is authenticated. Show Draft / Evidence Pending / Under Review / Returned / Verified / Expired / Suspended. Submit/Return/Approve uses current real CG-03 review decision when required, not self-approved browser field. A nurse may be associated with two centers via two separately reviewed affiliation rows; different center operator cannot view prior partner's evidence just because professional identity matches. **Admin Web/Internal Android:** Current approved Foundation reviewer has safe source summary and protected evidence review/action with same intent and result, readable outcome/reason and history. No automatic CG-10 employee creation.

### US-002 — Candidate shifts, leave and available time (no patient prerequisite)

**Center Portal:** Nurse Schedule with source-verified affiliate, weekday calendar/list, explicit IANA timezone when relevant, readable local start/end, overnight/holiday/leave exception and current effective period. Show 'Eligible working shift' separately from 'Available to assign' because another center could book this professional. Real admin and authorized Internal Android see current center-scoped availability and permitted review actions. A revised shift may not retroactively erase true booked occurrences; overlapping leave/request must show truthful conflict requiring original source authorization. No fake auto-generated patient bookings, no all-time calendar expansion or simulated current attendance. On OFF/revocation: no private menu or cached shift details.

### US-003 — Patient-consented nursing request and assignment

**Center Portal:** H13 **Create Nursing Booking** is an *internal authenticated center service request*, not public checkout. Select only real CG-15 consented eligible patient through purpose-limited minimal selector; one real CG-14 approved nursing center/service and accepted provider fee, center or private home-service location, approved visit frequency/duration/period and current qualified nurse. Reconfirm rate and patient care-purpose consent before final acceptance. Assignment UI shows nurse is qualified/affiliated for this real center and candidate local window; selecting a nurse does not guarantee booking until cross-center exclusion confirms. Clear user-facing `Requested`, `Under Review`, `Assigned`, `Declined`, `Reschedule Needed`, `Cancelled`. Source-safe message 'This nurse is unavailable at the selected time' on concurrency; never silently substitute another nurse or center. **Foundation Admin:** Actual scoped work/status only, no unrelated plan. **Own patient Web/native:** only narrow own confirmed status where legally and technically implemented, never new public Book/Pay CTA.

### US-004 — Care plan, protected actual visit and progress

**Center Portal H13 right-detail panel:** Tabs **Service Details**, **Nurse & Assignment**, **Care Plan**, **Visit & Progress**, **Documents** reflect real separately authorized source. Care Plan displays immutable version/timestamps and qualified author, protected minimum necessary content, reviewed vs draft state and explicit corrected revision. Assigned nurse with current appropriate clinical authorization records actual visit Started / Performed / Missed / Cancelled, time/place and source evidence through original CG-19 protected file service. Completion is never created by time passing or screen button alone; eligible completed service requires actual professional confirmation and applicable review. A correction/retraction is a new linked event, not deletion. When consent revoked or assignment changes while the tab is open, backend denies access and mobile/Web remove restricted content from view and device cache. User sees safe 'You no longer have access to this patient's care plan' without leaking another record exists. No generated prescriptions or clinical treatment suggestions.

### US-005 — Source-authoritative reports, reminder and optional CG-17 integration

**H13 Admin/Center status:** Cards distinguish genuine Request / Assigned / Scheduled / Started / Performed / Clinically Reviewed / Completed / Cancelled / Exception and verified real source-as-of. Pending or partially performed is not Completed; Completed is not Patient Paid, Foundation Approved or Foundation Settled. Actual center role can filter only own data by date/service/state; Foundation oversight only safe summary, not protected care notes. Optional source-approved real CG-17 benefit appears with separate `Not configured`, `Pending eligibility`, `Approved Foundation due`, `Actually settled` states **only when confirmed by original financial owner**, never mock H13 percentage, partner fees or automatic pay. Patient center money never becomes Foundation income. Real due-visit notification if opted in must show CG-19 confirmed delivery status, not a generated 'Sent' toast; no routine tenant scan while OFF or no due work. Internal Android same Foundation granted summary/approval as Admin Web; public Android own narrow status only.

## 3. Human-centered interaction and NFR design acceptance

**Screen behavior:** Visually identical permitted actions between Foundation Admin and Internal Android for the **same current role**; Center Portal partner operations do not imply an Internal Android partner account. Use actual names/service descriptions—not UUIDs, internal center codes, `patientRef` or server stack traces—in buttons, selectors, tables, notifications, errors, PDFs and TalkBack. Prefer inferred current center/actor defaults, short required forms, source-bound choices, no duplicate inputs for root/consent/grants. Current Harness Product UI Quality §4A applies to every changed UI, API, backend/database projection, not merely view code. Use responsive/tablet stacked detail, suitable keyboard focus, form error scroll/focus, accessible status/row/charts, 48dp-native tap targets, real Back/keyboard behavior, visual loading/empty/partial/ERP disabled/revoked/unknown save errors and reduced motion. Never show apparent success before server confirms original event.

**Privacy:** The same sensitive clinical note/file is forbidden in general Foundation Admin summary, unrelated Center B, Public Web/Android, logs, offline push, source picker and exports. Own patient details only through approved current CG-15 purpose and original CG-19 per-file authorization; avoid PHI in Android notifications/share sheets and clear protected cache on logout, revocation and role changes.
