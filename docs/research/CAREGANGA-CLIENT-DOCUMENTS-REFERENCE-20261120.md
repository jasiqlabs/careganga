## Client-policy discrepancies requiring confirmation before live issue

1. Certificate and welcome letter call the person **Active Member** while current membership selection includes Annual/Lifetime/Honorary/Student. Preserve **membership tier, active status and category** as separate configurable concepts; do not silently map these labels.
2. The sample welcome letter shows a one-off rupee fee and the certificate says **Lifetime (Subject to Policy)**. Neither amount, tenure, benefits, renewal or eligibility is a universal rate/policy.
3. Donation wording describes a **Section 8 Company** but the example stamp uses **Registered Trust**; the original materials also show differing registered offices/contacts across volunteer, member and donation documents. Corporate identity, address per document, tax treatment, stamp and signatory must be approved before issuance.
4. Donation receipt refers to **Temporary Receipt Details**, while email v2 demands receipt after provider-confirmed success. Clearly distinguish pending, temporary evidence (if authorized), and official provider-confirmed paid receipt. The sample states **80G not yet granted**; never promise 80G without current legal evidence.
5. Volunteer sample's public visible fields include phone and blood group. Public QR verification should expose only explicitly allowed minimum fields; optional medical/contact information stays private unless current policy permits it.
6. Password-provisioning template `CG-EMP-007` requires a secure one-use setup link, whereas current CG-01 baseline is Admin-created accounts with password and simple sign-in. **Do not silently alter auth flow**; reconcile this source conflict with the owner before implementing that trigger. No plaintext password in any email.
7. Proposed salary, employment terms, conditions, signature, membership price, logo and helpdesk phone in source artwork are examples, not sanctioned production defaults.
