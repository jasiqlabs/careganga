## What the original requirement actually means

CareGanga is **one Foundation root with many operating branches**, not one independent application/ledger/database per branch and not automatically a multi-NGO SaaS. Original CG-02 `module.md` BR02–BR10 and `dld.md` require independently approved/active branches, separate current managers, branch-scoped departments and history, and Foundation-wide functions. Original CG-01 identity grants current roles/capabilities. CG-02 owns branch identity; CG-04 members/volunteers, CG-05 projects, CG-09 finance/budgets, CG-10 employee assignments and other business owners must persist/enforce their own original branch association. There is no requirement to create a duplicate master or ledger for each branch. An explicit **app-wide persisted current-branch switcher** is not established by the approved CG-02 contract; it is not equivalent to authorization.

Correct business behavior:
1. A protected Foundation administrator sees Foundation-wide **and** all authorized active branch records, can explicitly narrow reports to a branch; a branch manager sees only currently assigned active branch(es), subject to the domain capability.
2. A Foundation-wide item (null branch) is not automatically owned by every branch. The original real root actor's authority determines who can edit/read it.
3. Reassignment, deactivation or a stale session must not bypass real current source-owner permissions; live actions must reject unauthorized/inactive branches and must not retroactively retarget previously posted finance/receipt history.
4. Public donors may use only currently eligible approved campaigns and their own 256-bit status proof; private donor/CG09 finance access is never inherited from a public CTA.
