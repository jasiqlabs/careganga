**Seven-stage form contract:** (1) real basic details/location/GPS/photo, (2) additional location/contact, (3) official photo/registration proof, (4) protected CG-19 agreement/supporting file, (5) eligible actual CG-01 manager, (6) select genuine **Foundation-wide CG-02 departments/committees** for link-on-activation and show CG-04/05/10/09 staff/project/budget options as explicitly unavailable until those owners deliver, (7) validate and **submit a frozen revision** to a real CG-03 case. Step summaries must identify missing file verification, invalid current geography/manager or inactive candidate department before saving/submitting, with an accessible correction action. Show Super Admin final decision separately from CG-02 confirmed activation. A manager assignment label by itself never implies newly granted root/branch permissions. Stage 6 does not require a not-yet-created branch-scoped department (it becomes attachable after branch activation). Do not invent names, beneficiary/staff lists, budgets or legal documents.

**Response states:** Show **Draft saved**, **Missing/invalid/revoked required evidence**, **Submitting—confirmation needed** (CG-03 case creation unconfirmed), **Submitted for review** (genuine case), **Returned for correction** (new frozen revision on resubmit), **Rejected with permitted reason**, **Final Super Admin decision recorded; activation pending**, **Activated with unique branch code** (verified CG-02 owner effect), **Old submission not applicable**, **Inactive/closed branch scope**, **Foreign geography/department denied**, **Outdated draft conflict**, **Upload pending/failed**, **No access**, **Network result unconfirmed**. Toast feedback may be transient but source detail retains the last verified state. On unknown submit/finalize outcome, refresh the real application before offering a duplicate mutation. A completed approval case is not interchangeable with an active branch until actual owner finalization.
