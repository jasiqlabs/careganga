## 3. Interaction storyboards and errors

**US-001:** select real doctor from H07 → see current CG-14 verified associations and active Center/Clinic dropdown → add/edit weekday local timezone windows (source sample ranges must remain blank until configured), duration/max slots, actual holiday and approved leave exceptions, capture changed effective policy, check cross-center doctor overlap and existing confirmed bookings before save. Distinguish mutually exclusive in-person center alternatives from forbidden cross-mode conflicts; the final booking still uses one doctor-global slot. Leave request pending/rejected/approved distinguished; a saved rule update never silently cancels an appointment. “Apply to Other Centers” opens confirmation listing only real eligible center mappings, per-target conflicts/permissions and effects; no fake Bulk Schedule success.

**US-002:** real CG-14 doctor/center profile/book CTA; center-first or doctor/time-first picker uses one availability API, with chosen doctor and center anchored across Back, calendar and payment review. If slot-first has multiple centers at the same time, show **explicit center names/address and center-specific price**; never pick first auto. If stale CG-14 fee, center suspension or slot conflict, show human-readable refresh and return to available choices. No patient registration required merely to browse.

**US-003:** privacy-oriented Patient Registration with approved displayed Virtual Patient ID only at actual accepted lifecycle; current verified mobile OTP from original CG-01 and separate consent checkbox/version, relevant optional BR10 data, optional private CG-19 document upload. A reception-assisted provisional patient cannot become a verified login simply by entering a phone; unresolved duplicate check asks authorized review, never auto-merge because of shared family phone. H15 protected patient view redacts unrelated clinical details; ordinary public cannot search a list by another person's patient ID.

**US-004:** doctor/center/location/mode/date/time and current **CG-14 full consultation fee** shown before editable final review. If CG-17 payment is not deployed or no center merchant eligible, single **Pay at Center** with amount due. When genuine CG-17 center merchant exists, “Online” may appear with partner center routing and verified return state (not Foundation gateway), with pending/unknown capture distinct from confirmed booking. Display precise “Booking Confirmed — Payment Due at Center” rather than “Paid” or “Foundation Benefit ₹…” unsupported. Double-booking race returns “This slot is no longer available” without dropping safe patient draft.

**US-005:** Booking ID + QR on real CG-19 issued slip and Add to Calendar from current confirmed appointment, safe share. Pay-at-center patient copy; actually paid-online one A4 patient+center copy policy; scanning public QR reveals only validity, center staff sees protected booking context only after role+own center auth; cancelled or forged code is invalid. Calendar contains real center, time and doctor but not symptoms, DOB/phone, report URL.

**US-006:** H15 consultation queue accessible by own-center receptionist: current date/location/doctor/table, check in, unique local queue token, waiting/consulting/completed/no-show, optimistic updates/reasons/actual previous status. Simultaneous two clerks use same token/status. This works with just authenticated booking identity, **not dependent on US-005 PDF/slip issuance**. Health documents cannot be globally exposed by queue display.

**US-007:** Cancel/Reschedule actions with who/why/fee history and deadline. Rescheduling retains prior booked slot until new one is atomically confirmed and patient accepts any real price difference; a failed second selection leaves original appointment alive. Paid booking uses actual CG-17 refund state only, otherwise old consultation amount due is display-only correction. CG-19 outgoing reminder/send status is separate from successful cancellation, and native/web both show accurate current appointment state.

**US-008:** H15 Admin patient right-detail **Booking History / Medical Profile / Documents** filtered strictly by permission. Public Patient profile shows My Appointments, Patient ID, real slips; Receipts only actual CG-17 verified sources, Reports only actual CG-16 professionally released files when delivered/authorized. **Prescriptions, Family Members and other future modules are hidden until separately approved**, not empty fake pages. Actual patient consent/access must be checked on download and across switching center/doctor, including screenshots/images, cached search results and export.
