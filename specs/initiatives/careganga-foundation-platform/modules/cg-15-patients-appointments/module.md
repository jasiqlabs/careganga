## 1. Core product and hard ERP/finance boundary

**One original healthcare partner-network platform**, not patient EMR or full hospital ERP. **CG-15 owns** patient business profile/virtual ID, verified identity linkage and consent evidence (CG-01 authenticates), doctor-center schedule/leave/exception/slot views, **one doctor-wide appointment occupancy/slot hold truth**, immutable booking and accepted doctor+center+fee snapshots, reception queue and cancellation/reschedule/visit source events. Patient can have several authorized center consultations without copying profile records between providers.

**CG-14 owns** real approved provider/location, one doctor identity, verified license/status and actual doctor↔center association, specialty/service, permitted consultation modes and effective **center-specific consultation fee**. CG-15 writes **no provider or doctor master** and never accepts booked center/doctor without a current eligible CG-14 source revision; CG-14 public directory works alone. CG-15 US-002 introduces genuine current `Book Appointment` CTA into real CG-14 provider/doctor detail and optional **already delivered** CG-18 approved editorial placements (CG-18 content works without CG-15; CG-15 no CMS table); original selected doctor, actual center/address and available time preserved.

**CG-01 owns** account/role/session/verified mobile challenge and provider/center staff access identity. **CG-15 owns only one patient/consent and restricted patient ID, not a duplicate login.** CG-19 owns protected file, appointment slip/document template and real notifications; QR/one-page print consumes original CG-19 issued source without CG-15 file store. **CG-03** owns actual approved doctor-leave or protected appointment exception reviewer decisions only when process required; CG-15 adds its real source-specific case adapter at the story that needs it, no generic approval table. **CG-17/CG-09** later own lawful center-collected actual money, Foundation support/benefit, online merchant payment, invoice/receipt, refunds, settlement and Foundation journal. **CG-15 first consultation booking uses real CG-14 approved full consultation price and `PAY_AT_CENTER / AMOUNT_DUE`**; can confirm when no CG-17 gateway/subsidy exists. **Never show "Paid Online", Foundation benefit, discount or invoice until real CG-17 policy/confirmed provider source exists.** The center's received cash is **not Foundation cash**. CG-17 later introduces its real booked-consultation finance integration, without rewiring CG-15 slot ownership or duplicate center bookings.

**All four optional ERP deployment switches** `CAREGANGA_ERP_LAB_ENABLED`, `CAREGANGA_ERP_PHARMACY_ENABLED`, `CAREGANGA_ERP_NURSING_ENABLED`, `CAREGANGA_ERP_OT_ENABLED` default false.
