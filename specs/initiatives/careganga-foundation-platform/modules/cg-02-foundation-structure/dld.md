## Data ownership and decision boundary

CG-02 owns exactly one legal/operational Foundation profile, its real geography tree, editable seven-step branch registration, the approved branch directory, and **Foundation-wide or branch-scoped** departments/committees. CG-21 owns provider/system configuration and may read the real CG-02 profile, not duplicate it. CG-01 owns authenticated actors; CG-03 owns approval decisions; CG-19 owns protected file bytes. A business actor, not a fabricated employee, may be linked during the initial branch draft. CG-10 employment/CG-05 projects/CG-04 memberships/CG-09 budgets introduce their own real links after they exist.

Only a completed authorized CG-03 decision may activate a branch and issue its unique code. Draft and approved data remain distinguishable, with one idempotent finalization for one registration. Store all cross-owner references as verified opaque keys; render readable business names, not identifiers.

## Canonical database and workflow contracts

- foundation_profile(id, foundation_root_realm_ref, legal_name, official_contact, postal_address, legal_registration_ref, version, updated_at): exactly one verified Foundation legal/contact master. DB UNIQUE(foundation_root_realm_ref) binds it to the real CG-01 Foundation realm (not per branch), with expected-version compare-and-swap, redacted source audit and restricted fields. CG-21 does not own this profile; CG-18 may later publish only approved fields.
- geography_area(id, level, parent_area_id, display_name, normalized_name, code, status, version): one canonical real state/district/(optional subdivision)/block/area hierarchy, not a second geography_unit alias. The DLD migration must distinguish normalized **root uniqueness** (e.g. a partial unique index on `(level, normalized_name)` where `parent_area_id IS NULL`) from normalized **child uniqueness** within the same parent (`parent_area_id, level, normalized_name`, for non-null parent). A simple `UNIQUE(parent_area_id,...)` does not protect roots in PostgreSQL. Validate allowed parent levels and self/cycle bans atomically, including concurrent reparenting; scoped, indexed selectors never invent locations.
- branch_registration(id, foundation_id, current_step, draft_version, status, requested_by, manager_account_ref, current_submission_revision, profile_payload, created_at, updated_at): one revisioned seven-stage **editable draft**, holding validated branch/name/type/opening date/geography/address/PIN/GPS/contact details, only actual CG-19 protected evidence references, eligible CG-01 manager and stage-6 selections of existing CG-02 Foundation departments. A submitted revision is frozen; editing requires RETURNED and a new draft revision, never mutation of previously submitted evidence.
- branch_registration_submission(id, registration_id, submitted_revision, immutable_snapshot_payload, immutable_payload_digest, file_evidence_ref_versions, submitted_at, approval_case_ref, status): **append-only frozen submitted snapshot**, never only a hash of mutable branch_registration.profile_payload. Store selected real CG-01 manager, geography, Stage-6 Foundation departments and CG-19 evidence identities/versions as actually reviewed; protect legal/PII snapshot reads. UNIQUE(registration_id,submitted_revision) and UNIQUE(approval_case_ref) where non-null; CG-03 case binds to the exact immutable source revision and digest. Returned correction writes a new frozen row/case preserving prior reviews. Missing/uncertain case creation is SUBMISSION_UNCONFIRMED until the original key resolves, not fake pending approval or duplicate case; rejected submission is terminal.
- branch(id, registration_id, geography_area_id, branch_name, branch_type, manager_account_ref, branch_code, status, version, activated_at, closed_at): UNIQUE registration_id and UNIQUE non-null approved business branch_code. Created once after real CG-03 approval using actual manager/geography, then managed via explicit active/inactive/closed transitions; never a provisional active row on draft.
- department(id, scope_kind, branch_id, parent_department_id, kind, name, normalized_name, opening_date, description, active, head_account_ref, version): one department/committee table; scope_kind FOUNDATION or BRANCH. A row-level `CHECK` enforces `(scope_kind='FOUNDATION' AND branch_id IS NULL) OR (scope_kind='BRANCH' AND branch_id IS NOT NULL)`; the **referenced branch being active** is a cross-row condition enforced under a transaction/appropriate locks plus an FK (not an impossible row-local CHECK). Same-scope parent validation/cycle prevention also needs locked transactional checks. Distinct partial unique indexes for Foundation rows (`kind, normalized_name WHERE branch_id IS NULL`) and branch rows (`branch_id, kind, normalized_name WHERE branch_id IS NOT NULL`) prevent NULL scope duplicates. Eligible real CG-01 accounts may head departments; CG-10 employment is linked later, not fabricated.
- department_participation(department_id, account_ref, office_role, active_from, active_until, created_by): one authorized membership/officeholder association of **real eligible CG-01 accounts** to a CG-02 committee/department, with appropriate unique active assignment and history; no CG-04 member/volunteer or CG-10 employee shadow master. Validate current actor and account scope; later modules introduce their own specialized relationships.
- branch_department_link(id, branch_id, foundation_department_id, linked_at, linked_by, ended_at, ended_by): authoritative **versioned association**, not a duplicate department. FKs to real branch and department plus partial UNIQUE(branch_id,foundation_department_id) WHERE ended_at IS NULL enforce at most one active relationship while permitting audited unlink/relink history. Verify referenced branch ACTIVE and department FOUNDATION-scoped under short locks: ordinary FK cannot express cross-row predicates. Stage-6 selected links are frozen in the submission and inserted only with activated branch, in the same owner transaction. A branch-scoped department cannot be created until real activation.

Commands: GET scoped geography/options, POST/GET/PATCH Foundation profile and department/committee participation and audited branch-department relations, POST/GET/PATCH branch-registration draft with expected draft_version, POST submit branch-registration (returns PENDING_APPROVAL **only after a real, revision-bound CG-03 case is confirmed**, otherwise SUBMISSION_UNCONFIRMED), GET current submission and immutable review history, and POST finalize-approved-registration (trusted CG-03-to-owner invocation only). Every write validates actor/capability/scope, expected revision and bounded source-idempotency key; all queries/exports/options are filtered server-side. Render readable business references, not raw implementation UUIDs.

**Submission and finalization boundary:** Lock CG-02 registration only for short save/submit transaction; check expected draft_version, freeze exact submitted snapshot (including genuine CG-19 file refs/versions), increment submitted_revision, then commit. **After commit**, call typed CG-03 create/find by immutable source key, exact revision and digest; on unknown result return SUBMISSION_UNCONFIRMED and query same key, never create a second case. For finalization, first authenticate a trusted CG-03 source invocation, obtain verified Super Admin final decision bound to that source revision/digest, and preverify CG-19 immutable evidence/status and CG-01 eligible manager **outside database locks**. Then a short CG-02 transaction locks registration/current submission and relevant Foundation/department/branch status rows; checks still-current revision, decision proof, evidence ref version and real scope, claims a unique effect key, allocates a DB-unique branch code, inserts one ACTIVE branch and its genuine Stage-6 links, writes redacted audit and commits. **No CG-01/CG-03/CG-19 owner facade or external provider I/O while a DB lock is held.** If preverified proof expired/changed, fail closed and refresh outside locks; do not assert distributed atomicity. A duplicate returns the existing source result; old approval/revoked evidence/foreign area/code collision cannot create another branch.

## Typed API and coordination

An authenticated `/api/foundation-structure` owner facade exposes business commands, filtered read-only queries, and minimal `options/search` projections for permitted selectors. Public reads require approved publication allowlist. Each PRD command contract:
- **FR-004** — CG-02 configures legal Foundation information, true geography, Foundation department/committee; US-002 adds genuine branch directory and post-activation branch-scoped department/link. Employee, volunteer, project and budget associations remain CG-10/04/05/09-owned and public publication is CG-18-owned. Validate current actor/real scope, version/idempotency and authoritative owner transitions.
- **FR-005** — Branch manager starts branch setup: Complete seven-stage draft/review/submit registration with contacts, location, documents, manager and linked resources; after required approval issue unique branch code. Validate current actor/real scope, source record/status, revision/idempotency key, and accept only a legitimate source-owner transition.

Business outcome categories: `SUCCESS/SUBMITTED_PENDING/VALIDATION/DUPLICATE/FORBIDDEN/NOT_FOUND_OR_HIDDEN/CONFLICT/UNAVAILABLE/UNKNOWN_WRITE_STATUS`. External provider failure never becomes fake success. Use a DB transaction for state+audited result, optimistic revisions or narrowly scoped row locks for races and unique source idempotency. Cross-owner effects go through typed facades, not another module's repository. Approved HLD pattern (plain state guard, policy, adapter, facade) only where actual variation justifies it; reject universal DSL, speculative microservice or duplicated ledger. No unbounded idle polling or uncontrolled retries.

**Folder/owner:** CG-02 is one new sibling at `apps/backend/src/modules/cg-02-foundation-structure/` under the **existing** NestJS composition root, with its own ordered tables in the one shared PostgreSQL migration history. **DB validity:** Use real FK to existing CG-01 accounts for manager/head/committee participants, but no FK to future CG-10 employees, CG-05 projects, CG-04 volunteers, CG-09 budgets or CG-18 CMS. Geography root/child and department Foundation/branch partial uniqueness are explicit; scope-kind/branch-present rules are row-local CHECKs, while **active referenced branch and parent/participant scope** are transactionally validated/locked. Keep cross-row history if a branch later closes: block new assignments, do not cascade-delete departments/old participation; transitions are audited and versioned. `branch(registration_id)` and approved `branch_code` are DB-unique; `(registration_id, submitted_revision)` and decision effect keys are also unique. Bounded collision retry must never issue two codes. Actual migration, rollback/forward-fix, Postgres version and any RLS decision remain unverified until implementation; no hardcoded sample records.

**One source-owned publication boundary:** Saving an internal Foundation identity, department or branch does **not** publish it. **Scope/rollback:** Queries and allowed option selections (including committee participants, geographic ancestors and branch-department links) are constrained to actual Foundation and branch contexts; do not require healthcare center/patient resource filters. A missing/lost response after submission/finalization is `UNKNOWN_WRITE_STATUS`; client refreshes real registration/case outcome rather than retries a new branch code. Reject foreign-area parent, cross-branch department parent, stale draft, insufficient evidence, unverified manager, invalid approval signature and forged callback with distinct safe categories. Every protected mutation writes one redacted audit event with its source transaction. On DB/API/provider failure, no partial branch code/active row or fictional approved state.

**No worker amplification:** Draft step saving and reviewer status loads are request-driven; CG-02 does not spawn a per-branch status poll/cron, image processor or document queue. CG-19 owns due-only protected file/notification processing; CG-03 owns real decisions. Zero pending branch work across idle browser/mobile sessions and multiple backend instances causes **zero periodic tenant/branch DB scans and external provider requests from CG-02**. Real requests use pagination, indexed filter/sort, bounded upload refs and finite retries (in the true owner only). Measure query/egress cost under authorized QA; state `NOT_MEASURED` until then.

**Human errors and structured observability:** The single existing safe error mapper returns `SUCCESS`, `DRAFT_SAVED`, `SUBMITTED_PENDING`, `RETURNED_FOR_CORRECTION`, `VALIDATION`, `FORBIDDEN`, `CONFLICT`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `UNKNOWN_WRITE_STATUS`; native/Web message catalog translates them, never prints raw IDs, SQL, exception or file tokens. Examples: “Draft saved.”; “Add the missing agreement before submitting.”; “This draft was updated elsewhere. Refresh to continue.”; “Your branch application was submitted for review.”; “We couldn't confirm submission. Check the application status before trying again.”; “You don't have access to this branch.” A business-safe reference/status appears in confirmation. Restricted logs link Web/Android action → typed API → CG-02 policy → DB transaction → CG-03/CG-19 calls through a redacted opaque correlation reference; never log legal IDs, private addresses, contact payloads or file bytes. Monitor bounded error, query/latency, conflict, file/approval failure and idle provider-call counters; no new observability stack.

**Acceptance/rollback:** Stage-by-stage save/resume and cross-client view are backed by exactly one source draft; each submission has its own immutable revision and CG-03 case. Returned branch corrections preserve previous CG-03 history and cannot accept stale decisions; Super Admin final decision authorizes only the matching current submission. One unique branch code and stage-6 department associations are committed under the same finalizer transaction; no duplicate activation/audit/links on concurrent callback. Branch closure never silently deletes historical department/committee membership. Independent CRITICAL schema review, isolated migration/rollback and responsive Web/Internal Android/source image/motion comparisons are **NOT_RUN** until authorized tests; no CI/GitHub Actions.

## PostgreSQL CRITICAL design, migrations and recovery (same repository)

- **Unique keys:** Foundation root has one legal profile with a unique real CG-01 root realm. Geography root uniqueness uses partial UNIQUE(level,normalized_name) WHERE parent_area_id IS NULL; children use partial UNIQUE(parent_area_id,level,normalized_name) WHERE parent_area_id IS NOT NULL. Normalize names consistently (case, whitespace and Unicode policy). Departments use row-local CHECK for FOUNDATION with null branch versus BRANCH with non-null branch, Foundation partial UNIQUE(kind,normalized_name) and branch partial UNIQUE(branch_id,kind,normalized_name), plus transactional parent/scope/active-branch checks. An FK cannot assert that the referenced branch is ACTIVE or a target department is FOUNDATION-scoped.
- **Cross-row locks/state:** Serialize geography reparent and ancestor/cycle changes on the real Foundation root lock before rechecking full ancestor paths. Branch department creation, link and ACTIVE to INACTIVE/CLOSED transitions share a fixed branch-row lock order and check current branch/department status in that transaction. Preserve history and prevent assignments on inactive/closed branches. CLOSED is not silently reopened; a future reopen rule needs approved change. For every submitted revision use immutable snapshot+digest, unique registration/revision, unique non-null CG-03 case ref, unique accepted decision effect and unique branch registration/code. Code generator may consume sequence gaps, never duplicate codes.
- **Constraints and links:** Genuine FK to existing CG-01 real accounts/realm and CG-02-owned parent/branch/department is allowed in the shared DB; never reference absent CG-04/05/09/10/14/18 tables. For officeholder participation, a partial unique active key under department/account/office_role prevents duplicate concurrent active assignments while retaining ended associations. For branch_department_link, partial unique active association plus ended_at history allows legitimate unlink/relink. Same-scope parent and valid active branch are enforced by guard+lock, not by a false row-local CHECK. CG-01 grants remain default-denied; naming a branch manager never silently grants broad access to a new branch.
- **Indexes and queries:** Stable capped cursor/page sort and scope-first filtering; candidates include area (parent_area_id,level,normalized_name), branch (status,geography_area_id,activated_at,id), department (scope_kind,branch_id,kind,active,normalized_name), registration (requested_by,status,updated_at,id), submission (registration_id,submitted_revision), participation (department_id,active_until,account_ref), active branch-department link (branch_id,foundation_department_id) and source audit (owner_ref,at,id). Tune actual composite indexes using real EXPLAIN and volumes; no speculative full-text/N+1 scan or uncapped export.
- **Migration/recovery:** Add only CG-02 owned tables to the already bootstrapped CG-01 single ordered PostgreSQL migration history. Validate engine/ORM/version and migration ordering in implementation; additive changes first, then enforce non-null/unique constraints after validating/backfilling real data when applicable. Verify empty-branch Foundation setup, concurrent unique/cycle cases, closure history and approval replay on an isolated disposable DB. Never blindly down-migrate/delete approved branches or historical decisions; use documented forward repair and independently verified restore when needed. DB review, migration run, rollback, EXPLAIN and restore remain NOT_RUN until genuine independent specialist/runtime work.
- **NFR/ops:** No per-branch polling/background worker: with zero eligible work across many branches/instances, CG-02 performs zero recurring tenant/provider scans. Measure separate DB queries, HTTP payload/egress, lock contention and approval/storage failure for busy and idle paths during authorized QA; runtime metrics NOT_MEASURED. Keep redacted accepted-change audit and restricted correlation without creating a second audit source. Preserve private Foundation/branch data; public CG-18 publication comes later with explicit approval.
