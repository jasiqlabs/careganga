## Five task-first flows and honest interactions

### US-001 — Verified nurse and center affiliation (H13)

Admin: source-safe nurse profile and review statuses `Draft / Submitted / Awaiting verification / Returned / Verified / Expired / Suspended`. For real current verified center, own qualified coordinator creates a professional profile with name, issuer/jurisdiction, registration reference, qualification and expiry, uploads original CG-19 restricted files, submits for the applicable genuine CG-03 pinned review when policy requires. Store file status `Pending validation` until actually verified; show current separate Center A/Center B affiliation approvals and review timeline. Only qualified independent reviewer may Approve/Return/Suspend; a Center A manager cannot inspect Center B private document or automatically activate a nurse in both centers. Human-readable nurse name + center + qualification, never ID/code in UI; do not add unnecessary Foundation ID, invented payroll employee or address field already server-derived.

### US-002 — Shift and real candidate availability (H13)

For current verified nurse affiliation, Center Portal offers calendar/week schedule, local IANA timezone, weekday and start/end, overnight indicator and bounded effective date range with leave/holiday exceptions. Available shift is **not** a confirmed visit; show `Eligible shift` with current source revision and `Unavailable due to other assignment` only when actual US-003 global reservations exist. Two centers may each list an authorized nurse's shift but must not book overlapping actual time; Admin/Internal current authorized role sees same state and meaningful safe conflicts. Avoid unsupported map/live tracking, fake 24-hour open or client-supplied unrestricted nurse ID.

### US-003 — Consented patient request, selected nurse and visit occurrence

H13 booking row and right detail show only permitted patient name/consent status, original CG-14 nursing service name, *accepted fee source*, care place kind, planned frequency/period, current state and assigned eligible nurse. Center operator selects actual consented CG-15 patient, preapproved nursing service, optional guarded home-location details, bounded date/recurrence, previews real availability and confirms one visit at a time with actual source-on-server conflict check. Differentiate `Requested`, `Awaiting consent`, `Accepted`, `Assigned`, `In progress`, `Canceled`, `Could not assign`. Confirmation cannot label `Paid` or create CG-15 doctor appointment. Race/lost request shows `Could not confirm yet—check the request before trying again`, never a success banner merely after tap. Public Web/Android show no nursing checkout/booking CTA; verified own-status self-service needs actual authorization and identical status semantics.

### US-004 — Private care plan and actual visit/progress

H13 right tabs contain bounded **Service Details**, **Nurse & Assignment**, **Care Plan**, **Visit & Progress**, **Documents** only for eligible care/team roles. Clinical notes encrypted; user sees only source-valid plan revision and actual assigned visit, privacy-first empty/denied states, no speculative patient-wide medical record. Nurse/care reviewer can Record Visit/Mark Missed/Request Correction/Attach Evidence after original CG-19 validation and right clinical permission. A queued/scheduled visit is not a performed visit; when nurse's credential, CG-14 location or CG-15 consent expires before action, deny safely. Original proof and correction timeline preserved; source auto-generates no patient diagnosis, drug advice, surgery procedure or billing. Native care-team interactions when truly assigned follow current clinical role and deliver Back/keyboard/photos/files permission without background location recording. No blind offline sync.

### US-005 — Accurate performance and original Foundation assistance

Show original-source `Total requests`, `Pending assignment`, `Assigned`, `Ongoing`, `Actual performed`, `Completed with review` for selected valid period/current center and source-as-of. Clearly separate `Completed nursing service`, `Patient payment (if CG-17 verified)`, `Foundation approved liability` and `Foundation actually settled`; never badge Completed as PAID or advertise H13 sample 30% rate. Policy missing → `No approved assistance for this service`. Current sensitive patient records are redacted in Foundation network summary; Center Portal reads only actual own patients and nurses with care purpose. Alerts use original CG-19 source; `Queued`, `Sent pending`, `Delivered`, `Failed` only from actual provider events. No delivery fallback to public push with patient name/location.
