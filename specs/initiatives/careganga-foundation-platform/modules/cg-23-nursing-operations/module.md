## Original scope and boundaries

**Foundation's verified partner nursing directory and genuinely published service/pricing/enquiry are CG-14-owned core services**, accessible with all ERP flags OFF and without any CG-23 nurse record. **CG-23 owns only enabled partner nursing operations**: verifiable nurse identity and credential evidence, center-specific affiliation, nurse shifts/availability, patient-consented request/assignment and actual visit schedule, restricted care-plan revision and visit/progress, safe operational aggregates and a conditional verified completion event into original CG-17. It does **not** own CG-14 provider/center/service/partner licence master, CG-15 patient/consent or doctor consultation booking, CG-19 file bytes/communications, CG-03 approval case, CG-01 roles, CG-10 employment, CG-11 payroll, CG-09 Foundation journal, CG-17 eligibility/patient collections/settlement or CG-20 source MIS. No general EMR, prescription/recommendation engine, public nursing checkout, family booking, direct insurance/transport, separate payroll/employment or speculative full offline-sync subsystem.

**Deployment safety:** `CAREGANGA_ERP_NURSING_ENABLED=false` in CG-01 typed process env. Missing/malformed env = OFF. Every CG-23 operational route/list/selector/export/command/background job requires **all**: live code and migrations + independent enablement gate; backend global ON; actual ACTIVE/VERIFIED CG-14 licensed nursing-capable center with current approved `NURSING_ERP` center grant; real CG-01 current role/action, account and assigned exact center; clinical license/qualified role/CG-15 patient consent when needed; genuine original owner record and current revision. The global switch never grants all centers or employees access. **OFF denies all CG-23 operational endpoints, including history/export, and stops jobs and new clinical writes without deleting immutable source records.** Only independently lawful/currently permissioned patient clinical release via original CG-15/19 may remain available, and original CG-21 protected audit may show minimal event metadata—never a hidden CG-23 history bypass. Current CG-14 public nursing enquiry remains unaffected.

**FR-087 financial guard:** Completed nurse visit `COMPLETED` is not a verified paid invoice or Foundation expense; CG-17 alone verifies eligible completed service, original accepted fee/approved support, and only CG-09 can post Foundation money. No automatic 30% nursing contribution from illustrative screenshots. CG-23 US-005 introduces an optional, narrow CG-23 completion adapter into the **existing** CG-17 owner when a real approved policy and actual completed service exist. Patient-collected money belongs to the center, not the Foundation. This integration is **not** required for CG-23 US-001–004 to work.
