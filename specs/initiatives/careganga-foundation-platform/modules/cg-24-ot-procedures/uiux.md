## 2. Five story-specific H14 and native journeys

### US-001 — Approved procedure information, current original CG-14 price and public enquiry

**Admin and Center Portal:** From verified center's **Procedure Master** view choose current genuine CG-14 approved service by human-readable procedure name/center. Edit only actual procedure-specific safe description, qualification/care prerequisites, estimated duration, preparation disclaimer and review status; **do not ask the user to type a second procedure fee, unbounded room list, patient or root ID**. Source-approved current fee/validity is fetched from CG-14, pinned for review and remains its only authority. Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Approved/Public → Returned/Withdrawn/Expired status as real. Show current published preview and a versioned history with qualified reviewer where real CG-03 policy requires. Changing CG-14 service price/center publication prompts current recheck, not silent stale promo.

**Public Web and native Android:** A real published hospital/procedure card can show authorized provider name, service name, currently verified CG-14 quoted fee, CG-24 approved safe description and estimated—not promised—duration, plus **Enquire/Contact** through actual CG-14 approved contact or genuine CG-19 support. Empty state ‘No published procedure information for this center yet’ rather than ₹0 or fictitious availability. Never show ‘Book OT now’, live private resource numbers, ‘30% CareGanga paid’, insurance approval or patient checkout based on H14 mockup. Public display and backend source review work with ERP OFF and without CG-15/17.

**Internal Android:** An actual Foundation source-reviewer who may approve/return the procedure-description revision in Admin Web has an equivalent native approval/return/history action (when this internal audience is served), or an explicit owner-approved exclusion before source completion. Native data/form is minimum and current; no clinical record is presented.

### US-002 — Real room, staff requirement and physical-equipment readiness (ERP ON)

**Center Portal:** **OT Rooms & Resources** list per actual authorized center, with readable room label, verified license/document status, equipment identity, availability/inspection state, qualification-based team requirements and current effective source. ‘Add Room’, ‘Record Readiness/Inspection’, ‘Mark Unavailable/Request Release’ and current-source ‘Assign Qualified Professional’ are separate protected actions, never a single editable `ready=true` toggle that overrides verification. A surgeon or anaesthetist is selected from real CG-14 global professional identity plus approved current center affiliation; one device moved between centers remains one physical asset identity. Show Draft / Evidence Pending / Verified Ready / Unavailable / Quarantined / Expired / Needs Review, with reason, source date and current qualified reviewer. Room listing is useful and testable before patient booking/US-003 exists.

**Foundation Admin/Internal Android:** Current Foundation oversight can verify/review readiness evidence and see safe center resource summary; internal native parity applies for identical reviewer capabilities. Nonclinical Foundation staff may not see unrelated clinical-only evidence. Failures from provider license, expired inspection, competing editor or missing CG-19 protected document must be shown as honest pending/conflict, never a green ‘Ready’ badge. No public asset list.

### US-003 — Protected patient OT request and atomic full-team/time booking (ERP ON)

**Center Portal H14 Create OT Booking:** Select actual CG-15 consented patient via purpose-limited ‘Search patient’, approved CG-14 procedure and real linked CG-24 procedure details, current center, planned date/time/estimated duration and required real room+surgeon/anaesthetist/qualified team+equipment. Require only decisions not already known from current authenticated center/role; use readable business labels rather than UUID, `resourceKey` or internal source revision. Display ‘Candidate slot’ until the single backend transaction successfully reserves **all** resources; only then show ‘Confirmed’. Partial/failure never marks case confirmed. Same surgeon associated with Centers A and B cannot appear available twice at overlapping times. One physical shared equipment item cannot be booked at two centers by different labels.

**States:** Proposed / Needs clinical approval / Resource unavailable / Confirmed / Reschedule pending / Cancelled / Review needed. On stale slot, concurrent conflicting booking or professional suspension, show ‘This room or team is no longer available at the selected time. Choose another time’ without leaking another patient's case. Reschedule keeps old confirmed reservation until the new full resource set is safely committed; cancel frees actual reserved resources once. Real timezone/day boundary/end-exclusive and optional source-verified turnover margin are visible; no invented travel margin or public promise.

**Overrun:** When a procedure remains IN_PROGRESS past booked end, the real assigned room/team/equipment is still occupied. A next case already scheduled for that resource must display **Needs review**, and its clinical start cannot be confirmed until real qualified release. Do not automatically make an occupied room ‘Available’ at midnight or after its slot end.

**Other channels:** Foundation Admin has only its actual scoped oversight, native Internal Android matching its same-role permissions, no public OT self-book/checkout. Public patient authenticated own release is an original CG-15/19 separate capability, not public private CG-24 route.

### US-004 — Clinically safeguarded pre-op, in-progress, post-op and documents (ERP ON)

**H14 selected-case detail:** Preserve Procedure Details / OT Team / OT Resources / Pre-op & Post-op / Documents plus real timeline. Cases display source-linked, versioned SCHEDULED, PRE_OP_READY, IN_PROGRESS, POST_OP, COMPLETED and honest CANCELLED/REVIEW_NEEDED branches. **The assigned actual clinical team** may confirm each phase under current patient consent and source readiness; general Center cashiers and Foundation admins do not get a clinical ‘Complete’ button. Clinical start claims actual live resource occupancy; signed completion with verified actual end frees occupancy. Scheduled end alone does not free resources or certify surgery.

**Documents & notes:** Original CG-19 protected validation, uploaded evidence status, signed review provenance and per-case notes remain restricted by patient and assigned clinical purpose. Corrected note or phase is a new linked immutable revision/event; no silent deletion/medical diagnosis generator. Screen survives careful role revocation or consent withdrawal by clearing patient data and showing safe ‘You no longer have access to this case’. Never expose patient diagnosis, home contact, original images or raw clinical text through public website, unrelated Center B or Foundation statistics.

**Mobile:** Internal Android action parity only for independently current qualified Foundation clinical actor where such capability is actually offered; otherwise oversight safe summary. A patient's public native app shows original lawful own released status only when original CG-15/19 channel supports it, not new OT case editor.

### US-005 — Real utilization reporting, due notifications and optional source finance

**H14 status KPI and utilization:** Center Portal and authorized Foundation Admin show real room-specific published readiness, bookings scheduled vs actual occupied, clinically in progress/completed/cancelled, correct time/center filters, actual original case source-as-of. Report only eligible records, distinguish source not available from source confirmed zero, never reuse demo Total OT Bookings or chart values. Clinical aggregate privacy, small groups and role scoping must remain safe. CG-20 later may consume source projections but is not required for current Center H14 reports.

**Financial state labels:** Completed clinical procedure ≠ Paid patient ≠ Approved Foundation liability ≠ Settled Foundation payment. Only when real CG-17 approves policy and accepted CG-14 service fee, a genuine signed CG-24 completed-source event may be sent once to CG-17 for its own independent financial validation. Show separately ‘No Foundation assistance configured’, ‘Pending approval’, ‘Center patient payment due’, ‘Foundation contribution approved’ or ‘Foundation paid’ as actually returned by source. No assumed 30% procedure discount or default partner commission. Patient money belongs to partner center, original CG-09 records Foundation-only obligations; a corrected/cancelled case posts only verified linked source correction.

**Alerts/background:** A real assigned case eligible for a bounded pre-op/room-readiness notification can send via original CG-19 outbox if consent/channel actually configured. ‘Sent’ only after CG-19 confirms, not from accepted queue. With no due rows, no provider/tenant scans or aggressive mobile polling; with OT ERP OFF, all CG-24 jobs/operational reports disabled.
