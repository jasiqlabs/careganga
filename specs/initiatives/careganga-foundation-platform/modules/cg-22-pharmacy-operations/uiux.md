## 2. Story-to-screen, complete UX and actual backend state

### US-001 — Price-only catalogue and review (H12)

**Admin Web:** H12-inspired active medicine price list with cards for genuinely derived approved/draft/expired values (only real if delivered), search by medicine name/generic/brand/pack, approved partner center and active/expired status, date and category filters, selected row right detail/drawer and **Create medicine**, **Propose price**, **Review/Return**, **Publish/Withdraw**, **View history** actions according to actual permissions. Form captures legal medicine display name, generic/brand/form/strength, supported pack and price unit, explicit currency/MRP/quoted price or validated discount with review start/end and source proof where needed. Before save, show recalculated same-pack quoted price and current effective period, clearly distinguish `Draft`, `Pending Review`, `Approved Not Yet Effective`, `Active`, `Expired`, `Withdrawn`, `Rejected/Returned`, `Conflict`. Historical accepted price never silently overwritten; show version timeline/reviewer safely.

**Center Portal:** Same own-center approved medicine and quote form but no global medicine editor or own-price self-approval if reviewer separation required. Show current center identity/approval status and permission-safe feedback; rejected revision editable only in a new draft.

**Public Next.js & Public Android:** Search only actually published approved center+medicine+effective rate, show named provider, normalized pack/strength, MRP/quoted price, business-safe discount/validity, and **Enquire/Contact center** only through actual CG-14 published contact or current legitimate CG-19 contact. When no rate, say `Price not available from this center` rather than zero; when source withdrawn, hide old price. No `In stock`, `Get medicine delivered`, `Add to cart`, user prescription upload or Foundation benefit chip without actual authorized source. No stock price change when ERP flag toggles.

**Internal Android:** Matching current authorised Foundation review actions, list filters, version detail, Review/Return/Approve with accessible native confirmation and source. If Web can approve price for that actor, native cannot silently omit the action; any explicitly excluded operation requires future owner approval, not an unexplained gap.

### US-002 — Inventory/batches and movement (H11, ERP ON)

Center Portal source list by real medicine, batch/lot, expiry, current `on hand`, `reserved`, `physical unreserved`, `issuable available` and `quarantined/unavailable` without treating reserved or expired/recalled physical stock as issuable. Editable source-labeled **Opening Stock** with actual proof, **Reserve**, **Release**, **Internal Stock Out**, **Request Adjustment**, and authorized **Approve Adjustment** are separate intentional actions, not generic `Edit total`. Show source unit/pack conversion and medicine/center location clearly, validate nonnegative units, and require confirmation for irreversible stock out/adjust. If a batch expires or is recalled while form is open, backend rejects and native/Web says `Batch no longer eligible for issue`, not `Success`. No anonymous or public stock counts.

Foundation Admin stock overview and current Internal Android oversight use same backend safe numbers and current effective grant; if ERP OFF, business stock menus/actions/active projections disappear and immutable prior stock records remain in database while every CG-22 operational/history/export route stays OFF; original CG-21 may expose separately permitted minimal audit-event metadata. Center Portal role revoked during form submission shows `Access changed, refresh` and no stale movement result.

### US-003 — Supplier, purchase and goods receipt (H11, ERP ON)

Center Portal submenu **Suppliers → Purchases → Goods Receipts**, filter own-center/status/date, current legal supplier and approved purchase lines, purchase details + accepted vs outstanding units, protected evidence state, partial receipt wizard and history. Distinguish `Requested`, `Under Review`, `Approved`, `Partially Received`, `Received`, `Rejected`, `Canceled` and `Evidence Pending`. A supplied document pending CG-19 validation is not stock received. Show real invoice/purchase valuation as **partner pharmacy cost information**, not Foundation payment or beneficiary charge. When partial GRN accepted, show actual accepted/rejected quantities, remaining amount and one original batch movement. Unsupported supplier account/private bank number never renders in public or unrelated Foundation UI.

### US-004 — Transfer and in-transit stock (H11, ERP ON)

Center Portal **Transfer Stock** from actual Source Pharmacy Location A to current approved **same legal partner's** destination B. Show batch manufacturer/lot/expiry/available and active grants for *both* centers, eligible stock units and destination confirmation. One source-user `Dispatch` means `In Transit` and **does not** increase destination. Authorized destination receiver sees `Received`, `Partially Received`, `Rejected/Returned`, `Loss Pending Review`, `Reconciled` with original movement/source reference and actual residual in-transit. A 20-unit dispatch followed by distinct verified receipt events for 12 then 5 units must show cumulative 17 received and 3 still in transit, never counted at both centers or overwritten under one receipt ID. Conflict, double-receive, revoked grant, wrong destination or ERP flipped OFF while transit show truthful blocked/reconciliation-needed state. Nothing claims courier location/GPS tracking from screenshot alone.

### US-005 — Expiry, quarantine, recall and operational reports (H11, ERP ON)

H11 inventory overview offers current real `Near Expiry`, `Expired`, `Low Stock`, `Quarantined`, `Recalled` filter panels and batch detail/history. Actual threshold/date/source labels are visible; overdue alert is not an issued e-mail until original CG-19 confirms it. `Quarantine`, `Recall`, `Request Release`, `Approve Release` require current actor/reason/evidence and second approval where policy demands; never ordinary release of expired or unresolved recalled batch. Show applicable sites with verified affected lot and current independent center permission, with controlled recall history and no public patient data. Center Portal own operations and Foundation Admin authorized summary/exception actions map to same backend. Native Internal Android current eligible Foundation oversight includes accessible batch status, reason/history/review action if currently available on Web, not a second Center Portal.
